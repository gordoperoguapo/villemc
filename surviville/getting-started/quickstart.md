# Parkour



Level 1

***

#### 🧗 Level 1 – Master Strafing

Strafing is **key** to beating this level. Once you leap onto the larger pillars, line yourself up on the ladder and begin strafing **slowly and carefully** around the pillar as you climb. Keep circling upward until you reach the top - then make a clean jump to the next level.

💡 **Tip:** Smooth movement beats speed here. Rushing will send you back down.<br>
