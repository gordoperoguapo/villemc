# Ender

### **Ender**

The End is Skyville's boss arena. Fight the Ender Dragon, defeat it, and earn an Ender Key. Each key opens a special crate at the `/ender` spawn with exclusive rewards.\
<br>

***

### **How It Works**

✅ **/ender** - Teleport to the End spawn.\\

Once you arrive, head to the dragon arena and take on the Ender Dragon. Defeat it to receive an **Ender Key**.

Take your Ender Key back to the special crate at the `/ender` spawn and open it for your rewards.

***

### **Ender Keys**

⭐ You earn one Ender Key each time you defeat the dragon.\
⭐ Keys open the special Ender crate at the `/ender` spawn.\
⭐ The crate contains exclusive rewards you can't get anywhere else.\\

***

### **Tips**

⭐ Bring your best gear. The dragon hits hard.\
⭐ Team up with island members to take it down faster.\
⭐ Save your keys or open them right away. The choice is yours.\\
