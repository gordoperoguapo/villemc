# Player Vaults

### **Player Vaults**

Player Vaults give you extra personal storage that follows you everywhere. Think of them as bonus chests only you can access. Open them with `/pv` or `/vault`.\
<br>

***

### **How It Works**

✅ **/pv** - Open your vault selector.\
✅ **/pv** - Open a specific vault directly (for example `/pv 1`).\
✅ **/vault** - Same as `/pv`.\\

Click a vault in the selector to open it. Store anything you want in there. Your items are safe and accessible from anywhere.

***

### **How Many Vaults You Get**

Your rank determines how many vaults you can use:

⭐ **Adept** - 10 vaults.\
⭐ **Sage** - 15 vaults.\
⭐ **Oracle** - 35 vaults.\
⭐ **Luminary** - 50 vaults.\\

Higher ranks automatically include everything from lower ranks.

***

### **Tips**

⭐ Use vaults to organize by category. One for building blocks, one for gear, one for valuables.\
⭐ Vaults are perfect for storing items you need across different islands or sessions.\
⭐ Your vaults are private. No one else can access them.\\
