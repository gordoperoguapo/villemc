# The Pit

### **Welcome to The Pit**

The Pit is Skyville's combat zone. It is where envoy crates land, nightmare mobs spawn, and players fight for loot. PVP is on, keep inventory is on, but god mode is disabled. Come prepared.

***

### **Getting There**

* ✅ **/pit** - Teleport to The Pit.
* ✅ **/the pit** - Same thing, whichever you remember.

***

### **Envoys in The Pit**

Envoys are server-wide loot events where supply crates drop for everyone to race toward and claim. On Skyblock, they land in The Pit.

When an envoy starts, a broadcast goes out and crates begin falling. First come, first served. Run to the nearest crate and right-click it to claim your reward. Each crate gives one reward when claimed and then disappears.

There are three crate tiers. Rarer tiers spawn less often but have better loot:

* ✅ **Basic Crate** (Chest) - Most common. Cash, crate keys, vbits.
* ✅ **Lucky Crate** (Ender Chest) - Uncommon. Bigger cash, more keys.
* ✅ **Titan Crate** (Enchanting Table) - Rare. The best rewards.

Envoys run automatically every hour with countdown warnings at 30 minutes, 15 minutes, 10 minutes, 1 minute, and a final 30-second countdown.

***

### **Nightmare Mobs**

The Pit is crawling with nightmare mobs. Kill them for cash payouts. They hit hard, so bring good gear and don't go in naked.

***

### **The Rules**

* ⭐ **PVP is ON** - Other players can attack you. Watch your back, especially during envoy events when everyone is fighting over crates.
* ⭐ **Keep inventory is ON** - You keep your items when you die. Dying still stings, but you won't lose your gear.
* ⭐ **No god mode** - /god does not work in The Pit. Everyone bleeds the same.

***

### **Tips**

* ⭐ Show up early for envoy events. The best crates go to whoever gets there first.
* ⭐ Bring a team. Numbers win crate races and keep other players off your back.
* ⭐ Farm nightmare mobs between envoy events for steady cash.
* ⭐ Keep inventory means you can fight without risking your best gear, so don't be afraid to engage.
