# Island Commands

### **Island Commands**

Everything you can do with your island is controlled through the `/is` command. This page covers every player-facing island command.\
<br>

***

### **Getting Started**

✅ **/is create** - Create a new island.\
✅ **/is home** - Teleport to your island.\
✅ **/is go** - Teleport to your island (same as /is home).\
✅ **/is spawn** - Teleport to spawn.\\

***

### **Teleportation**

✅ **/is sethome** - Set your island home location.\
✅ **/is warp** - Teleport to one of your island warps.\
✅ **/is setwarp** - Create a warp point on your island.\
✅ **/is delwarp** - Delete one of your island warps.\
✅ **/is warps** - List all warps on your island.\
✅ **/is visit** - Visit another player's island.\\

***

### **Team Management**

✅ **/is invite** - Invite a player to your island.\
✅ **/is kick** - Remove a player from your island.\
✅ **/is leave** - Leave your current island.\
✅ **/is team** - Open the team management menu.\
✅ **/is members** - List all members of your island.\
✅ **/is promote** - Promote a team member to a higher role.\
✅ **/is demote** - Demote a team member to a lower role.\
✅ **/is transfer** - Transfer island leadership to another member.\
✅ **/is expel** - Remove a visitor from your island.\
✅ **/is ban** - Ban a player from visiting your island.\
✅ **/is unban** - Unban a player from your island.\\

***

### **Island Settings**

✅ **/is settings** - Open the island settings menu.\
✅ **/is rename** - Change your island's name.\
✅ **/is description** - Set your island's description.\
✅ **/is open** - Open your island to visitors.\
✅ **/is close** - Close your island to visitors.\
✅ **/is lock** - Lock your island (same as close).\
✅ **/is toggle border** - Toggle your island border visibility.\
✅ **/is biome** - Open the biome selection menu.\
✅ **/is chat** - Toggle island team chat.\\

***

### **Flight and Movement**

✅ **/is fly** - Toggle flight on your island (requires Oracle rank or higher).\\

***

### **Economy**

✅ **/is bank** - Open the island bank menu.\
✅ **/is top** - View the top islands by level.\
✅ **/is level** - Check your island's level.\
✅ **/is worth** - Check your island's worth.\
✅ **/is rate** - Rate another player's island.\\

***

### **Upgrades and Missions**

✅ **/is upgrade** - Open the island upgrades menu.\
✅ **/is missions** - View your island missions.\
✅ **/is panel** - Open the island control panel.\\

***

### **Danger Zone**

⭐ These commands are permanent. Be careful.\\

✅ **/is disband** - Delete your island permanently.\
✅ **/is reset** - Reset your island back to its starting state.\\

***

### **Tips**

⭐ Use **/is settings** to control exactly what visitors and each team role can do.\
⭐ Set warps for your farm, shop, and other key areas so members can get around fast.\
⭐ Your island level goes up as you build and add valuable blocks. Check **/is top** to see how you rank.\\
