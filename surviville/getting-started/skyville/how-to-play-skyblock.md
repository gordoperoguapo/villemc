# How to Play Skyblock

### **Welcome to Skyville**

Skyblock is all about starting from a small floating island and building it into something massive. You gather resources, complete missions, upgrade your island, recruit a team, and climb the leaderboard. This guide covers the core island gameplay from your first block to the endgame.

***

### **Creating Your Island**

Type **/is create** and pick your starting island. You get three choices:

* ✅ **Normal** - The classic grass island. Balanced start.
* ✅ **Desert** - Sand and cactus theme. Different resource mix.
* ✅ **Mycelium** - Mushroom island. Unique blocks from the start.

Your island starts small (20 blocks in each direction from center). A glowing border shows your boundary. Toggle it with **/is toggle border**.

Island names can be 3 to 16 characters and support color codes. Your name shows up on the island leaderboard.

Changed your mind? You can disband and start over up to 5 times. Your inventory and ender chest are kept, so you lose nothing but the island itself.

***

### **Your Team and Roles**

Islands are better with friends. Your team starts at **4 members** and can be upgraded all the way to **32**.

**Roles** (each role gets everything below it plus more):

* ⭐ **Member** - Build, break blocks, use chests, open the island panel, fly on the island, place spawners, deposit money into the island bank.
* ⭐ **Moderator** - Invite and kick members, open or close the island to visitors, set warps, withdraw from the island bank.
* ⭐ **Admin** - Promote and demote members, change the biome and island settings, manage coop players.
* ⭐ **Leader** - Full control over everything.

**Coop players** (up to 8) can help build on your island without being full members. Good for temporary helpers.

**Guests** start with no permissions. Open your island to visitors and place a \[Welcome] sign so people can find you with **/is visit**.

Your island has a floating hologram above spawn showing the owner, island level, bank balance, biome, size, and member count. Left-click it to open the island panel. Right-click to open settings.

***

### **Missions**

Type **/is missions** to see your mission tracks. There are five tracks, each with five tiers. Finish a tier to get paid automatically, then move on to the next.

* ✅ **Miner** - Mine stone, cobblestone, and ores. The very first mission just asks you to place an ice block to build a cobblestone generator (pays $1,000). Finishing Miner tier I also upgrades your generator one level for free.
* ✅ **Farmer** - Plant and harvest crops. Early tiers want carrots and potatoes. Rewards include seeds and golden apples.
* ✅ **Slayer** - Kill hostile mobs like skeletons, creepers, zombies, and spiders.
* ✅ **Fisherman** - Catch fish. Rewards include a Beginner's Rod to get you started.
* ✅ **Explorer** - Unlock new dimensions. The first tier pays **$50,000** plus obsidian and fire charges just for unlocking your Nether island.

Missions only count progress while you are on your island.

***

### **Island Upgrades**

Type **/is upgrades** to spend money on permanent island improvements. There are 12 upgrade tracks:

* ⭐ **Island Size** - Grow from 20 radius all the way to 125 radius. Costs $30K, $80K, $150K per tier.
* ⭐ **Generator** - Improve your cobblestone generator with better ores. Costs $25K, $75K, $150K, $500K. See the Generator section below for what each tier adds.
* ⭐ **Team Members** - Raise your team cap from 4 to 8, 16, then 32. Costs $100K, $300K, $500K.
* ⭐ **Crop Growth** - Speed up farming from x1 to x4. Costs $100K, $500K, $1M.
* ⭐ **Spawner Rates** - Boost mob spawner output from x1 to x4. Costs $500K, $1M, $2.5M.
* ⭐ **Mob Drops** - Multiply mob loot from x1 to x4. Costs $1M, $2M, $5M.
* ⭐ **Hopper Limit** - Go from 8 to 64 hoppers. Costs $1M, $3M, $9M.
* ⭐ **Minecart Limit** - Go from 4 to 32 minecarts. Costs $1M, $3M, $9M.
* ⭐ **Villager Limit** - Go from 2 to 10 villagers. Costs $50K, $150K, $300K.
* ⭐ **Bee Limit** - Go from 5 to 50 bees. Costs $100K, $250K, $1M.
* ⭐ **Fox Limit** - Go from 5 to 50 foxes. Costs $100K, $250K, $1M.
* ⭐ **Piston Limit** - Go from 500 to 5,000 pistons. Costs $50K, $100K, $250K, $500K.

***

### **The Cobblestone Generator**

Your generator starts simple: 95% cobblestone, 5% coal ore. Upgrade it to unlock better ores:

* ✅ **Level 1** ($25K) - Adds Iridium Ore at 1%
* ✅ **Level 2** ($75K) - Adds Iron Ore at 3%
* ✅ **Level 3** ($150K) - Adds Lapis at 4%, Iridium at 2%
* ✅ **Level 4** ($500K) - Adds Gold at 3%, Diamond at 2%, Emerald at 1%
* ✅ **Level 5** (max) - Adds Redstone at 9%, Ancient Debris at 1%, Iridium at 4%

**Iridium Ore** is special to Skyville. You need a **diamond pickaxe or better** to mine it. Anything weaker and you get nothing.

***

### **Island Level and the Leaderboard**

Every block on your island has a worth value. Your island's total worth divided by 2 is your **island level**. Type **/is top** to see where you rank.

Blocks that boost your level fast:

* ⭐ **Iron Golem Spawner** - Worth 10,000. The single biggest level boost in the game.
* ⭐ **Diamond Block** - Worth 300
* ⭐ **Emerald Block / Gold Block** - Worth 150 each
* ⭐ **Dragon Egg** - Worth 150
* ⭐ **Beacon** - Worth 100
* ⭐ **Iron Block** - Worth 10

Basic blocks like cobblestone and dirt are only worth 1 to 2, so the real climb comes from spawners and valuable blocks.

***

### **Minions**

Minions are worker helpers you place on your island. They farm, mine, chop, fish, fight, or sell for you automatically.

* ✅ **8 types:** Miner, Slayer, Collector, Feeder, Farmer, Fisher, Lumberjack, Seller
* ✅ **Limit:** 3 minions per player by default (ranks can place more)
* ✅ Link them to a chest within 30 blocks and they deposit what they collect
* ✅ The **Seller** minion automatically sells items for cash

Two things to know: minions **do not work while you are offline**, and they are disabled in the End dimension.

There is also a Minion Crate with upgrades to make them stronger.

***

### **Making Money**

* ⭐ **Shop** (/shop) - Buy and sell through the GUI shop. Sell prices are 25% of buy prices. Big sellers: Netherite Block $562.50, Emerald Block $225, Diamond Block $112.50, Gold Block $56.25.
* ⭐ **Sell All** (/sellall) - Instantly sells everything sellable in your inventory.
* ⭐ **Sell Wands** - Right-click a chest with a sell wand to sell everything inside at once. Tier I and Tier II available.
* ⭐ **Player Market** (/market) - Buy and sell with other players.
* ⭐ **Auction House** (/ah) - List items for auction.
* ⭐ **Missions** - Direct cash payouts. Explorer tier I alone pays $50,000.
* ⭐ **Pay** (/pay) - Send money to other players. Check the richest with /baltop.

There is also a credits currency called **vbits** (/vbits) shared across the whole network.

***

### **The Dragon Fight**

Skyville has a custom dragon arena separate from island End dimensions.

Type **/ender** to reach the End area, or **/dragon** to go straight to the arena. When the dragon is summoned, everyone on the server gets notified. You have **5 minutes** to bring it down.

This is a damage race. The player who deals the **most damage** wins **3 Reward Keys**, announced server-wide. Bring your best gear.

***

### **Ranks and Island Perks**

Four ranks, each stacking on the last:

* ✅ **Adept** - Anvil, condense, disposal, ender chest, feed, recipe lookup, colored nicknames, 10 personal vaults, Adept kit.
* ✅ **Sage** - Back, heal, repair, personal time and weather, stonecutter, loom, nearby player check, 15 personal vaults, Sage kit.
* ✅ **Oracle** - **Island flight** (/is fly), island settings access, sit and bellyflop, 35 personal vaults, Oracle kit.
* ✅ **Luminary** - **God mode** (/god), see other players' inventories, 50 personal vaults, Luminary kit.

Everyone gets **/fly** as a shortcut for island flight.

### **Tips for New Players**

* ⭐ Do your Miner missions first. Tier I upgrades your generator for free, which speeds up everything else.
* ⭐ Save up for the Explorer tier I Nether unlock. $50,000 is a huge early boost.
* ⭐ Iridium needs a diamond pickaxe. Don't waste time trying with iron.
* ⭐ Iron Golem spawners are the fastest way to climb the island leaderboard.
* ⭐ Team up early. More members means faster missions, bigger builds, and shared resources.
* ⭐ Check /is top regularly to see what the top islands are doing differently.
