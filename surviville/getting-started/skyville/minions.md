# Minions

### **What Are Minions**

Minions are worker helpers you place on your island. They mine, farm, fight, fish, and sell for you automatically while you are online. Right-click a placed minion to open its control panel. Left-click to pick it back up.

Two things to know up front:

* Minions do **not** work while you are offline.
* Minions cannot be used in the End dimension.

***

### **The 8 Minion Types**

* ✅ **Miner** - Mines the block directly in front of it every 2.5 seconds. Handles Iridium correctly, drops go to your linked chest. Great for stone, ores, and obsidian.
* ✅ **Slayer** - Attacks hostile mobs in its radius every 7 seconds and collects their drops plus XP. Burns through health the fastest, so keep it fed.
* ✅ **Collector** - Vacuums up dropped items within 8 blocks every 5 seconds. Perfect at farm collection points.
* ✅ **Feeder** - Automatically feeds and heals your other minions using food from linked chests. Essential when running multiple minions.
* ✅ **Farmer** - Harvests and replants crops every 3 seconds. Works with wheat, carrots, potatoes, and more.
* ✅ **Lumberjack** - Chops trees and replants saplings automatically every 10 seconds. Works with all wood types.
* ✅ **Fisher** - Catches fish from nearby water every 3 seconds. Can find treasure and junk too. The most durable minion, needs feeding the least.
* ✅ **Seller** - Automatically sells items from its linked chest for cash every 15 seconds. Turns your farms into passive income.

***

### **How to Get Minions**

There are two ways to get a minion: craft one or win cores from the Minion Crate.

**Crafting**

Each minion is crafted at a crafting table using 4 identical **minion cores** in a diamond pattern:

* Top middle, middle left, middle right, bottom middle
* All 4 cores must be the same color

Each minion type needs its own core color:

* ⭐ **Miner** - Orange-red core
* ⭐ **Slayer** - Lime green core
* ⭐ **Collector** - Royal blue core
* ⭐ **Feeder** - Deep pink core
* ⭐ **Farmer** - Aquamarine core
* ⭐ **Lumberjack** - Bright orange core
* ⭐ **Fisher** - Purple core
* ⭐ **Seller** - Dodger blue core

Minion cores look like small 3D potion bottles in your inventory.

**Minion Crate**

Open Minion Keys at the crate area for a chance at cores and fuel. Every reward has an equal chance:

* 8 minion cores (one for each type)
* Aluminum Dust (minion fuel)

***

### **The Full Loop**

1. Get cores from the Minion Crate
2. Craft 4 matching cores into a minion at a crafting table
3. Place the minion on your island
4. Right-click it, hit Link Chest, then right-click a chest within 30 blocks (you have 30 seconds)
5. Feed it Aluminum Dust to keep its health up
6. Upgrade it through the control panel to make it faster and better

***

### **Feeding Your Minions**

Every minion has 20 health. They lose 1 HP after a set number of actions, so they need regular feeding to keep working.

* ✅ **Fuel:** Aluminum Dust (from the Minion Crate)
* ✅ **How:** Right-click the minion, hit Feed Minion, and it eats 1 dust from your inventory for +5 HP
* ✅ **Shortcut:** The Feeder minion auto-feeds your other minions if it can reach food in a linked chest

Some minions drain faster than others:

* ⭐ **Slayer** - Needs feeding most often (loses 1 HP every 4 actions)
* ⭐ **Miner / Lumberjack** - 1 HP every 10 actions
* ⭐ **Collector / Feeder / Farmer / Seller** - 1 HP every 15 actions
* ⭐ **Fisher** - Needs feeding least often (1 HP every 20 actions)

***

### **Control Panel**

Right-click any placed minion to open its panel:

* ⭐ **Change Skin** - Customize how your minion looks
* ⭐ **Feed Minion** - Spend Aluminum Dust to restore health
* ⭐ **Link Chest** - Connect a chest within 30 blocks for auto-deposit
* ⭐ **Minion Health** - See current health out of 20
* ⭐ **Upgrade Minion** - Spend money to level it up for better speed and efficiency (check the button in-game for the next level's cost)
* ⭐ **Rotate** - Change which way the minion faces (matters for Miner placement)
* ⭐ **Rename** - Give it a custom name up to 16 characters

***

### **Placement Limits**

You can place up to **3 minions** by default. Plan your setup around that cap.

***

### **Tips**

* ⭐ Start with a Miner and a Seller. The Miner gathers, the Seller turns it into cash.
* ⭐ Add a Feeder early if you run more than one minion. It pays for itself in saved feeding trips.
* ⭐ Use Rotate on your Miner to aim it at the exact block you want mined.
* ⭐ Link chests before you walk away. Unlinked minions drop items on the ground.
* ⭐ The Fisher is the lowest maintenance minion, good for a fire-and-forget slot.
* ⭐ Slayers are hungry. Keep Aluminum Dust stocked or pair one with a Feeder.
