# Auction House

### **Auction House**

The Auction House is Skyville's player marketplace. List your items for sale, browse what others are selling, and bid on auctions. It is the best way to turn your extra loot into cash.\
<br>

***

### **How It Works**

✅ **/ah** - Open the Auction House.\
✅ **/ah sell** - List the item in your hand for sale.\
✅ **/ah search** - Search for a specific item.\\

To sell, hold the item you want to list and type `/ah sell <price>`. Your item goes live for other players to buy.

To buy, open `/ah` and browse the listings. Click an item to purchase it instantly.

***

### **Tips**

⭐ Check prices before listing. Undercutting gets you faster sales.\
⭐ Rare items and enchanted gear tend to sell well.\
⭐ The Auction House takes a small fee on sales, so factor that into your pricing.\
⭐ You can cancel your listing and get your item back if it does not sell.\\
