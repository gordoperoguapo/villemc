# Market

### **Market**

The Market is Skyville's central trading hub. Shopkeepers here will trade your Raw Iridium for building blocks and materials. If you need concrete, wool, dye, or glass for your island, this is where you get it.\
<br>

***

### **How It Works**

Each shopkeeper offers a simple trade: **1 Raw Iridium** gets you **16 blocks** of their material. Walk up to a shopkeeper, right-click to open their trade menu, and click the trade to exchange your iridium for blocks.

Raw Iridium is earned through gameplay on Skyblock. Save it up and bring it to the Market when you need building supplies.

***

### **What You Can Get**

**Concrete** - All 16 colors. Perfect for clean, modern island builds.\
**Wool** - All 16 colors. Great for soft textures and pixel art.\
**Dye** - All 16 colors. For crafting and coloring.\
**Stained Glass** - All 16 colors. For windows and decoration.\
**Glass** - Plain glass and tinted glass for builds.\\

Each trade gives you **16 blocks** for **1 Raw Iridium**.

***

### **Tips**

⭐ Stock up on Raw Iridium before a big building project so you can grab everything in one trip.\
⭐ Concrete and wool come in every color, so plan your palette ahead of time.\
⭐ The Market is the fastest way to get bulk building materials without grinding for each block type.\\
