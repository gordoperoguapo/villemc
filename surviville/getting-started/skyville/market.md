# Market

### **Market**

The Market is Skyville's central trading hub. Shopkeepers here will trade your iridium for building blocks, keys, rare items, and more. If you need supplies for your island, this is where you get them.\
<br>

***

### **How It Works**

Walk up to a shopkeeper and right-click to open their trade menu. Each shopkeeper offers different trades in exchange for iridium (raw iridium or iridium ingots). Click the trade to exchange your iridium for their goods.

Iridium is earned through gameplay on Skyblock. Save it up and bring it to the Market when you need supplies.

***

### **Building Blocks**

The basic material shopkeepers offer bulk trades on building blocks. These trades give you **16 blocks** for **1 iridium**:

✅ **Concrete** - All 16 colors. Perfect for clean, modern island builds.\
✅ **Wool** - All 16 colors. Great for soft textures and pixel art.\
✅ **Dye** - All 16 colors. For crafting and coloring.\
✅ **Stained Glass** - All 16 colors. For windows and decoration.\
✅ **Glass** - Plain glass and tinted glass for builds.\\

***

### **Keys and Rare Items**

Other shopkeepers offer crate keys, rare items, and specialty goods. Trade amounts vary by item, check each shopkeeper's menu to see their deals.

⭐ Crate keys for unlocking reward crates.\
⭐ Rare items you can't easily get elsewhere.\
⭐ Specialty goods for island upgrades.\\

***

### **Tips**

⭐ Stock up on iridium before a big building project so you can grab everything in one trip.\
⭐ Concrete and wool come in every color, so plan your palette ahead of time.\
⭐ The Market is the fastest way to get bulk building materials without grinding for each block type.\\
