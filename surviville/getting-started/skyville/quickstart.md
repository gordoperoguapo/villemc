# Island Teams

### **Island Teams**

Skyville islands are built to be shared. Invite other players, assign them roles, and control exactly who can do what on your island. Every member contributes to your island's worth and level, so a good team is one of the best ways to grow.\

### **How It Works**

Every island has one Leader and can hold additional members up to your current team limit. New members join by accepting an invite and are assigned the Member role by default. From there, the Leader or an Admin can promote them through the role ladder.

Each role inherits all the permissions of every role below it. A Moderator can do everything a Member can, plus more. An Admin can do everything a Moderator can, and so on. You never need to manually grant lower permissions to a higher role — they already have them.

Your starting team limit is 4 players including yourself. You can expand this through the island upgrade menu.

***

### **Roles**

Roles run from lowest to highest. Each one includes everything the role below it can do.

**Member** — The default role for anyone who joins your island. Members can build, break blocks, access chests, fly on the island, deposit money into the island bank, break spawners, use the island chest, and purchase island upgrades.

**Moderator** — Trusted players who help manage visitors and the island's public presence. Moderators can ban and unban visitors, expel players, open and close the island to the public, create and delete warps, withdraw money from the island bank, and break valuable blocks on top of everything a Member can do.

**Admin** — Senior members who help run the island itself. Admins can invite and kick members, promote and demote players, add and remove coops, set the island biome, set the island home, change island settings, and edit island permissions on top of everything a Moderator can do.

**Leader** — Full control. There is only one Leader per island. The Leader can do everything, including disbanding the island and transferring leadership to another member.

***

### **Coop and Visitors**

**Coop** — A coop player is not a full member but has been granted temporary build-level access. Coops can build, break, interact, fish, and farm, but cannot manage the island or its members. Coop access is automatically removed when no eligible members are online, so it is best used for active sessions rather than permanent trust.

**Visitor** — Anyone who visits your island without being a member or coop. Visitors have no permissions by default. You can open the island permissions menu to selectively enable specific actions for visitors, such as interact, fish, or entity ride, without giving them full membership.

***

### **Commands**

Managing your team

✅ `/is invite <player>` — Send an island invite. The player must accept with `/is accept`. Requires Moderator or above. \
✅ `/is kick <player>` — Remove a member from your island. Requires Moderator or above. \
✅ `/is promote <player>` — Promote a member one step up the role ladder. Requires Admin or above. \
✅ `/is demote <player>` — Demote a member one step down. Requires Admin or above. \
✅ `/is setrole <player> <role>` — Set a member's role directly. Requires Admin or above. \
✅ `/is members` — Open the member panel to see everyone on your island and their current roles. ✅ `/is leave` — Leave an island you are a member of. Leaders must transfer ownership before leaving. \
✅ `/is transfer <player>` — Transfer leadership to another member. You will remain on the island as an Admin.

Coop

✅ `/is coop <player>` — Grant a player temporary coop access. Requires Admin or above. \
✅ `/is uncoop <player>` — Remove coop access from a player. Requires Admin or above. \
✅ `/is coops` — View all players currently coopped to your island.

Visitors and access

✅ `/is accept` — Accept a pending island invite. \
✅ `/is ban <player>` — Ban a player from visiting your island. Requires Moderator or above. \
✅ `/is pardon <player>` — Unban a previously banned player. Requires Moderator or above. \
✅ `/is expel <player>` — Immediately teleport a visitor off your island. Requires Moderator or above. \
✅ `/is open` — Open your island to the public so anyone can visit. \
✅ `/is close` — Lock your island. Only members and coops can enter.

***

**Tips and Good to Know**

⭐ Each role inherits all permissions from the roles below it so you never need to manually grant lower permissions to higher roles. \
⭐ Your island worth and level count every block placed by any member, so an active team is one of the fastest ways to climb the island top. \
⭐ Coop access is automatically revoked when no eligible members are online. Use it for active sessions, not as a permanent trust solution. \
⭐ Use `/is close` to lock your island while you are away. Your members and coops can still get in but random visitors cannot. \
⭐ If you want to step down as Leader, use `/is transfer <player>` first. You will automatically become an Admin and stay on the island. \
⭐ You can expand your team slot limit through the island upgrade menu. Check there for the current cost to unlock more member slots.
