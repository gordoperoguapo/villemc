# Island Missions

Island missions are your island's to-do list. Open the missions menu with **/is missions** and work through tasks on your own island: mining, farming, fishing, fighting, and growing your island level.

There are **67 missions** in six chapters. When you finish a mission, the reward pays out automatically. The menu shows your live progress on every mission, with a percentage and a counter for each requirement, so you always know how close you are.

***

### **Getting Started**

Getting Started is the onboarding path: 16 missions that teach you the island basics, from your first cobblestone generator to your first trip to the Nether. Finish it and you are set up for everything else.

Three free ranks are earned along the way:

* ⭐ **Spark** - Earned at Tool Up, the fourth mission.
* ⭐ **Wisp** - Earned at Monster Hunter, the eighth mission.
* ⭐ **Nova** - Earned at Graduation, the final mission.

Your first two generator speed upgrades are also granted here, by First Dig and Iron Age. The chapter pays **$150,000** in total.

* **First Steps** - Place an ice block and build your first cobblestone generator.
* **First Dig** - Mine 64 cobblestone and stone from your generator.
* **Fuel Up** - Mine 32 coal ore.
* **Iron Age** - Mine 32 iron ore.
* **Tool Up** - Craft a furnace and a set of iron tools. Earns the Spark rank.
* **First Harvest** - Grow and harvest 32 wheat.
* **Green Thumb** - Grow and harvest 64 sugar cane and 32 pumpkins.
* **First Catch** - Catch 10 fish of any kind.
* **Monster Hunter** - Kill 25 hostile mobs. Earns the Wisp rank.
* **Smelter** - Craft 8 hoppers.
* **Enchanter** - Enchant 5 items at an enchanting table.
* **First Brew** - Brew 6 potions. Any basic type counts.
* **Gold Rush** - Mine 24 gold ore and 32 redstone ore.
* **Diamond Standard** - Mine 16 diamond ore.
* **Nether Bound** - Create your Nether island.
* **Graduation** - Grow your island to level 10,000. Earns the Nova rank.

***

### **Miner**

The Miner chapter opens after Iron Age, the third Getting Started mission. All 12 missions are generator mining, working up from cobblestone and coal to the best blocks a maxed out generator can make. The chapter pays **$265,000** in total.

* **Miner I** - Mine 48 cobblestone or stone and 16 coal ore.
* **Miner II** - Mine 96 cobblestone or stone, 32 coal ore, and 16 iron ore.
* **Miner III** - Mine 64 coal ore and 64 iron ore.
* **Miner IV** - Mine 192 coal ore and 320 iron ore.
* **Miner V** - Mine 32 diamond ore.
* **Miner VI** - The generator makes lapis now. Mine it in bulk.
* **Miner VII** - Emeralds, the rarest standard ore in the generator.
* **Miner VIII** - Gold ore from the generator.
* **Miner IX** - Pure volume: 5,000 stone from the generator.
* **Miner X** - Redstone, which only generates on a maxed out generator.
* **Miner XI** - Iridium, the purple ore in your generator.
* **Miner XII** - Ancient debris, the last block the generator can make.

***

### **Farmer**

The Farmer chapter opens after Green Thumb, the sixth Getting Started mission. It covers every crop you can grow on the island, including a nether wart farm, and ends with a thousand of everything. The chapter pays **$415,000** in total.

* **Farmer I** - Plant a small farm to start your journey.
* **Farmer II** - Expand your farm with beetroots, pumpkins, and melons.
* **Farmer III** - Expand your farm with sugar cane and cactus.
* **Farmer IV** - Every crop you know, a full stack of each.
* **Farmer V** - Five stacks of every crop you grow.
* **Farmer VI** - Nether wart grows on soul sand. Start the nether farm.
* **Farmer VII** - Every crop you grow, 640 of each.
* **Farmer VIII** - The nether farm at full scale: nether wart at 640.
* **Farmer IX** - Sugar cane and cactus, 2,000 each.
* **Farmer X** - The row crops: carrot, potato, wheat, and beetroot, 1,000 each.
* **Farmer XI** - Pumpkins and melons, 2,000 each.
* **Farmer XII** - Every crop on the island, a thousand of each.

***

### **Fisherman**

The Fisherman chapter opens after First Catch, the seventh Getting Started mission. It mixes everyday catches, rare fish, and treasure fishing. The chapter pays **$235,000** in total.

* **Fisherman I** - Catch one of each fish: cod, salmon, tropical fish, and pufferfish.
* **Fisherman II** - Fish up 5 treasure or junk items. Also pays a Rare Crate Key.
* **Fisherman III** - Catch rare treasure in the deep ocean.
* **Fisherman IV** - 100 common fish, 50 rare fish, and 5 enchanted books.
* **Fisherman V** - 500 common fish, 250 rare fish, and 25 treasures.
* **Fisherman VI** - Cod and salmon, 1,000 each.
* **Fisherman VII** - The rare fish: pufferfish and tropical fish.
* **Fisherman VIII** - Prime treasure off the hook: 15 pieces.
* **Fisherman IX** - Every fish at double the last count: 2,000 cod, 2,000 salmon, 1,000 pufferfish, and 1,000 tropical fish.
* **Fisherman X** - 50 enchanted books and 50 nautilus shells.

***

### **Slayer**

The Slayer chapter opens after Monster Hunter, the eighth Getting Started mission, the same mission that earns the Wisp rank. It runs from your first hostile mobs through Wither fights and Ender Dragon kills. The chapter pays **$471,250** in total.

* **Slayer I** - Kill 5 hostile mobs.
* **Slayer II** - Kill 32 skeletons, 32 zombies, 32 spiders, and 24 creepers.
* **Slayer III** - Blazes, endermen, and wither skeletons. Prepare for the boss fights.
* **Slayer IV** - Summon the Wither and kill it.
* **Slayer V** - Kill the Ender Dragon.
* **Slayer VI** - The overworld four: zombies, skeletons, spiders, and creepers, 250 of each.
* **Slayer VII** - Blazes and wither skeletons from the nether island.
* **Slayer VIII** - Endermen on the end island, 250 of them.
* **Slayer IX** - Summon the Wither three times. Kill it three times.
* **Slayer X** - 1,500 kills across every mob you know.
* **Slayer XI** - The Ender Dragon, three more times.
* **Slayer XII** - Five withers and two dragons. Everything, at once.

***

### **Explorer**

The Explorer chapter opens after Graduation, so it is the post-onboarding chapter. It is a pure island level ladder: build your island bigger and better, and each rung pays more than the last. The chapter pays **$595,000** in total.

* **The-End Explorer** - Create your End island.
* **Island Master** - Reach island level 25,000.
* **Island Legend** - Reach island level 50,000.
* **Island God** - Reach island level 100,000.
* **Island Eternal** - Reach island level 250,000, the top of the ladder.

***

Add up all six chapters and a full mission clear pays **$2,131,250**. Start with Getting Started, keep an eye on the counters in /is missions, and the rest of the chapters open up as you go.
