# Ranks

## Adept

#### **Commands:**

✅ **/anvil** - Instantly repair your items on the go.\
✅ **/condense** - Convert raw materials into their compact forms with ease.\
✅ **/disposal** - Open a convenient GUI to clear out unwanted items.\
✅ **/enderchest** - Access your ender chest anytime, anywhere.\
✅ **/beezooka** - Launch a bee at your target-whether friend or foe, we won't judge.\
✅ **/kittycannon** - Is someone getting catty? Send an explosive feline their way.\
✅ **/nick** - Customize your in-game name with full hex and gradient support.\
✅ **/recipe** - Instantly check any crafting recipe without searching online.\
✅ **/feed** - Keep your hunger bar full without carrying food.

#### **Additional Perks:**

⭐ Custom chat prefix to display your rank.\
⭐ Adept kit with exclusive gear.\\

**Sage**
--------

#### **Commands:**

✅ **/back** - Instantly return to your previous location.\
✅ **/heal** - Fully restore your health in an instant.\
✅ **/loom** - Craft banners anytime, anywhere, when creativity strikes.\
✅ **/near** - See which players are nearby-no more surprise visits.\
✅ **/ptime** - Set your personal time of day, just the way you like it.\
✅ **/pweather** - Customize the weather in your own world space.\
✅ **/repair** - Instantly fix damaged items to keep them battle-ready.\
✅ **/stonecutter** - Carry a portable stonecutter for on-the-go building.

#### **Additional Perks:**

⭐ Custom chat prefix to display your rank.\
⭐ Sage kit with exclusive gear.\
⭐ Everything from Adept.\\
