# Jobs

## Jobs

Jobs are a way to earn money and points by doing things you already do while playing. Mine blocks, chop trees, cook food, fish, explore the world and get paid for it automatically. Open the jobs menu with `/jobs` or `/j` to browse and join.

***

### How It Works

Each job pays you money for completing specific actions related to that job. As you work you also earn job XP which levels up your job up to a maximum of Level 200. Higher levels mean better payouts. You can hold multiple jobs at once up to your allowed limit.

Each job in the menu shows how many players are currently working it, your current level, and your current XP so you can always see where you stand.

***

### Available Jobs

**Blacksmith** - Earn money by repairing items.\
**Destroyer** - Earn money by breaking all types of blocks.\
**Alchemist** - Earn money by brewing potions.\
**Builder** - Earn money by placing and building with blocks.\
**Chef** - Earn money by cooking and crafting food.\
**Crafter** - Earn money by crafting all kinds of items.\
**Demolishing** - Earn money by blowing up blocks with TNT and explosives.\
**Excavator** - Earn money by digging dirt, gravel, sand, and similar blocks.\
**Enchanter** - Earn money by enchanting weapons, tools, and books.\
**Explorer** - Earn money by exploring new areas of the world.\
**Farmer** - Earn money by growing and harvesting crops, breeding and milking animals.\
**Fisherman** - Earn money by fishing.\
**Hunter** - Earn money by killing players, animals, and villagers.\
**Taster** - Earn money by eating all kinds of food.\
**Smelter** - Earn money by smelting items in a furnace.\
**Miner** - Earn money by mining ores and minerals.\
**Sheepherder** - Earn money by feeding and shearing sheep.\
**Tamer** - Earn money by taming and breeding animals.\
**Trader** - Earn money by trading with villagers.\
**Gunsmith** - Earn money by crafting weapons and bows.\
**Woodcutter** - Earn money by chopping trees and logs.

***

### Jobs Points Shop

As you work your jobs you also earn points alongside your coins. Spend your accumulated points in the Jobs Shop on exclusive rewards. Click the Jobs Shop button inside the /jobs menu or type `/jobs shop` to browse what is available.

***

### Commands

✅ `/jobs` - Open the jobs menu to join, leave, and view your job stats.\
✅ `/jobs shop` - Open the jobs points shop.\
✅ `/jobs join <job>` - Join a job directly by name.\
✅ `/jobs leave <job>` - Leave a job.\
✅ `/jobs stats` - View your jobs statistics.

***

### Tips and Good to Know

\
⭐ Each job levels up to 200 so there is always room to grow your payout rate.\
⭐ The Explorer job pays you just for moving through uncharted chunks so it pairs well with any other activity.\
⭐ Spend your job points in `/jobs shop` regularly since they accumulate over time.\
⭐ Check how many players are in each job from the menu. Jobs with fewer workers may indicate less competition for resources.
