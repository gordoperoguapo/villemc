# Teleport

## Teleportation

Surviville has two teleportation systems. The RTP menu lets you explore new areas across all four dimensions, and the player teleport system lets you request teleports to and from other players.

***

### Random Teleport

Type `/rtp` to open the Random Teleport menu. Pick a dimension and you will be dropped at a random location within that world's border. PvP is on in all RTP destinations so be ready when you land.

#### Overworld RTP

Teleports you to a random location in the main survival world.

Border: 15,000 x 15,000\
Cost: Free\
PvP: On

#### Nether RTP

Teleports you to a random location in the Nether.

Border: 10,000 x 10,000\
Cost: $5,000\
PvP: On

#### End RTP

Teleports you to a random location in the End.

Border: 10,000 x 10,000\
Cost: $10,000\
⚔PvP: On

#### Resource World RTP

Teleports you to a random location in the Resource World. This world is reset periodically so it is the best place to mine and gather materials without affecting the main world.

Border: 10,000 x 10,000\
Cost: Free\
PvP: On

***

### Player Teleportation

You can request to teleport to other players or invite them to teleport to you using the standard teleport request system.

#### Commands

✅ `/tpa <player>` - Send a teleport request to another player. They must accept before you are moved.\
✅ `/tpahere <player>` - Request that another player teleports to you.\
✅ `/tpaccept` - Accept an incoming teleport request.\
✅ `/tpdeny` - Decline an incoming teleport request.\
✅ `/tpcancel` - Cancel a teleport request you sent before it is accepted.\
✅ `/tpauto` - Toggle automatic acceptance of all incoming teleport requests.\
✅ `/back` - Return to your last location before a teleport. _(Radiant rank and above.)_

***

### Tips and Good to Know

⭐ Use the Resource World for all your mining and bulk gathering. It resets regularly so resources are always fresh and the main world stays clean.\
⭐ PvP is active in all RTP destinations so land with your eyes open, especially in the Nether and End.\
⭐ The Nether and End RTP cost coins so make sure you have enough before clicking.\
⭐ Teleport requests expire after a short time if not accepted so retype your request if it times out.\
⭐ Use /tpauto if you are playing with friends and want to skip the accept step for incoming requests.
