# Fishing

## Custom Fishing

Fishing on Surviville is not your average Minecraft fishing experience. Every catch involves a skill-based minigame, fish come in different quality tiers, and the rod you use and where you fish both affect what you reel in. Whether you are fishing casually or grinding for coins, there is a lot to know.\

### Getting Started

1. Grab a rod from /shop under the Fishing category.
2. Head to any body of water, lava, or a cave.
3. Cast your line and wait for a bite.
4. Complete the minigame to reel in your catch.
5. Sell your fish with /sellfish.

Fish come in three quality tiers. Normal fish sell at base price, Silver★ fish sell for 1.5x, and Gold★★ fish sell for 2.5x. Higher quality fish always trigger harder versions of the minigame so better gear helps.

Use /fishingbag to store your catches and gear, and /sellfish to open the Fish Market and sell everything at once.

***

### Where to Fish

Location determines what you catch and which minigame you play.

**River / Freshwater** - Goldfish, Perch, Mullet, Carp, Catfish, Woodskip, and Sturgeon. Also Rainbow Fish and occasional junk. Minigame: Click Bar.\
**Ocean / Saltwater** - Tuna, Pike, Sardine, Octopus, Sunfish, Red Snapper, and Jellyfish. Also Apple Crate, Carrot Crate, and Kelp. Minigame: Tension Bar.\
**Swamp** - Special swamp fish with a bonus loot table. Minigame: Click Bar.\
**Underground (Y: -64 to 0)** - Cave fish available as bonus catches.\
**Lava (Bone Rod required)** - Overworld lava gives Cobblestone, Stone, Obsidian, and Diamond. Nether lava gives Netherrack, Gold, Magma Cubes, and lava fish. Fishing at night with a Bone Rod can attract Skeletons, and Wither Skeletons in the Nether. Minigame: Hold Game.

***

### Fishing Rods

All rods are available in /shop under Fishing.

✅ **Beginner's Rod** (64 uses) - Longer wait time and an easier minigame. Great for new players.\
✅ **Silver Rod** (96 uses) - Adds +8 Silver★ group weight per cast.\
✅ **Golden Rod** (80 uses) - Adds +3 Gold★★ group weight per cast.\
✅ **Star Rod** (128 uses) - Adds +15 seconds of minigame time.\
✅ **Bone Rod** (32 uses) - Required to fish in lava. Attracts Skeletons at night and Wither Skeletons in the Nether.\
✅ **Magical Rod** (16 uses) - Catches enchantment books only. Requires Book bait and 30 EXP levels. Consumes 1 EXP level per cast.\
✅ **Master's Rod** (128 uses) - Faster bites and a harder minigame. Adds +5 Silver★ and +3 Gold★★ weight. The endgame rod.

***

### Bait

All bait is available in /shop under Fishing.

✅ **Simple Lures** - Reduces minigame difficulty by 10. Best for players who struggle with the minigame.\
✅ **Magnetic Lures** - Bites come 0.9x faster and adds +2 seconds of minigame time.\
✅ **Wild Lures** - Increases difficulty by 20 and reduces minigame time by 2 seconds, but applies a 1.5x size multiplier to catches. High risk, high reward.\
✅ **Book** (vanilla item) - Required bait for the Magical Rod only. Has no effect on any other rod.

***

### Minigames

The minigame you play depends on where you are fishing.

🎣 **Click Bar** (River / Freshwater) - A marker moves across a bar. Click at the right moment to land it in the highlighted zone. Gold★★ fish use the hardest variant.\
🎣 **Tension Bar** (Ocean / Saltwater) - Hold your mouse to build tension. Do not let it go too high or too low. Gold★★ fish use the hardest variant.\
🎣 **Hold Game** (Lava) - Hold a key for the required duration. Gold★★ fish are significantly harder.

Use Simple Lures to lower difficulty or the Star Rod to get more time to react.

***

### Hooks and Enchantments

✅ **Delicate Hook** (16 uses) - Adds +1 Silver★ and +1 Gold★★ group weight. Attach it to your rod. Remaining uses are shown in the rod's lore.\
✅ **Luck of the Sea I** - Adds +2 Silver★ and +1 Gold★★.\
✅ **Luck of the Sea II** - Adds +4 Silver★ and +2 Gold★★.\
✅ **Luck of the Sea III** - Adds +6 Silver★ and +3 Gold★★.\
✅ **Lure I / II / III** - Reduces wait time when fishing in lava or the void by 80 / 160 / 240 ticks.

For maximum Gold★★ odds, stack Luck of the Sea III on a Golden Rod with a Delicate Hook attached.

***

### Totems

Build totem structures near your fishing spot to buff everyone in range.

⭐ **Double Loot Totem** - Lasts 5 minutes, radius 8 blocks. Doubles all loot caught nearby. Build the structure, then hold a Nautilus Shell and interact to activate it. Consumes the shell on activation.\
⭐ **Golden Star Totem** - Lasts 2 minutes, radius 10 blocks. Adds +15 Gold★★ weight for all nearby anglers. Build the structure, then hold a Gold Ingot and interact to activate it. Consumes the ingot on activation.

Active totem time is shown in your action bar. Combine totems with quality rods for the best possible Gold★★ sessions.

***

### Selling Your Catch

Use /sellfish to open the Fish Market GUI. Place fish in the slots and click Sell, or use Sell All to clear your inventory and fishing bag at once.

Sell prices follow the formula: Price = Base + (Bonus x Size). Bigger fish are worth more coins, and Gold★★ fish have higher base and bonus values than normal.

There is a daily earning cap of $10,000. Once you hit it the market will deny further sales until the daily reset.

***

### Fishing Competition

Weekend fishing tournaments run on Saturday and Sunday with rounds at 9:30, 14:30, and 20:00. Each round lasts 5 minutes and requires at least 2 players to start. The goal is simple: catch the most fish during the round.

🥇 1st Place - $200\
🥈 2nd Place - $100\
🥉 3rd Place - $100\
Participation - $10

During competition weekends bite time is 15% faster across the board, so it is a great time to grind.

***

### Tips and Good to Know

⭐ Higher quality fish always trigger harder minigames so investing in a better rod makes a real difference.\
⭐ Your fishing bag stores rods, bait, hooks, and catches. Sell All empties your bag too.\
⭐ Totems stack with rod bonuses so activate one before a competition round for maximum output.\
⭐ The Magical Rod only works with Book bait and drains EXP per cast so keep your levels up.\
⭐ Gold★★ fish are worth 2.5x base price and are significantly harder to reel in.
