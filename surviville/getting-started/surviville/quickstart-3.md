# Farming

## Custom Farming

Grow crops. Sell produce. Build your fortune.

VilleMC features a fully custom farming system. Unlike vanilla Minecraft, these crops cannot be mega-farmed or automated. Every harvest requires active player involvement, making farming one of the most rewarding and fair ways to earn money on the server.\

***

### How It Works

1. Buy seeds from /shop under the Farming section.
2. Plant seeds in a Planting Pot, keep them watered, and let them grow.
3. Harvest your crops by right-clicking with an empty hand when fully grown.
4. Sell your produce at /shop for coins and reinvest into your farm.

Seeds are investments. They cost significantly more than a single crop sells for. You need 2 to 7 harvests to break even, then every harvest after that is pure profit. The more you reinvest into pots and seeds, the faster your income compounds.

***

### Seasons

Each season lasts 28 in-game days, cycling through Spring, Summer, Autumn, and Winter. Most crops only grow in specific seasons. Plant outside their window and they will wither and die. Planning around the season calendar is the most important skill a farmer can develop.

Garlic and Apple Tree grow in any season. They are your year-round income backbone that keeps coins flowing even when seasonal options are limited. Always keep some Garlic plots running.

***

### Getting Started

#### Step 1 - Buy a Planting Pot

Every crop needs a Planting Pot ($250 each) to grow in. Head to /shop and pick some up. Pots absorb rainwater automatically and can also be filled with a Water Bucket (+3 water) or Glass Bottle (+1 water). Each pot holds one fertilizer at a time.

Save up for 16 pots ($4,000) as your starting plot. That is enough to generate steady income with Garlic while you build toward bigger things.\
\
**Hard limit of 50 pots per player**.

#### Step 2 - Buy Seeds

Seeds are available in /shop under Farming. For your very first farm, start with Garlic. It grows in any season, always drops exactly 4 crops per harvest, and seeds become self-sustaining after just 2 harvests. Once you have capital, branch into higher tiers.

#### Step 3 - Plant, Water, and Wait

Right-click your Planting Pot with seeds to plant. Pots absorb rainwater passively, or you can water manually with a bucket or Watering Can from /shop. Crops grow through multiple visible stages and are ready to harvest when fully grown.

Invest in a Sprinkler when you can. Even a Sprinkler I ($50,000) covering 5 pots removes the need to manually water those plots. Sprinkler III ($750,000) covers 24 pots from a single placement and is a major long-term goal for serious farmers.

#### Step 4 - Harvest and Sell

Right-click a fully grown crop with an empty hand to harvest. Each harvest drops Normal, Silver Star, or Gold Star quality. Higher quality means a higher sell price. Take your produce to /shop and sell for coins. Use Quality fertilizers to increase your chances of premium drops.

***

### Crop Reference

Silver Star crops sell for 1.5x the Normal price. Gold Star crops sell for 2.5x the Normal price. Use Quality fertilizers to increase your chances of rolling Silver and Gold.

#### Common

Best starter crops with low seed costs, guaranteed seed returns, and consistent yields. Seeds become self-sustaining in 2 to 3 harvests.

Garlic is the best starter crop in the game. It grows year-round, always drops exactly 4 crops per harvest, and seeds pay themselves back quickly. These crops will be the foundation of your income for a long time.

#### Uncommon

Bigger investment, better returns. Higher seed costs but stronger long-term income once established.

Hops and Apple Tree are special. Once you buy the seed you never need to buy again as they replant themselves after every harvest. The higher seed cost is a one-time barrier, not a recurring expense.

#### Rare

For established farmers with capital to invest. Higher yields and better per-harvest returns, but some crops require patience.

Pineapple is deceptively powerful. It always returns exactly one seed meaning zero ongoing cost after the first purchase. $90 per harvest with zero seed costs adds up very quickly once established.

#### Exotic

Winter-only crops with no seed return and the highest reward ceiling in the game.

Red Packet Tree is the crown jewel of VilleMC farming. Winter-only, 5 growth stages, and no seed return. You pay $250 every single harvest. In return it drops 1 to 4 crops per harvest at $120 each, up to $300 for Gold Star. Prepare your plots and stock up on seeds before Winter arrives.

***

### Tools and Equipment

Tools are one-time permanent purchases from /shop. Upgrading your tools is a long-term goal. Better tools mean faster watering, more pots covered, and more time harvesting instead of managing.

✅ **Watering Can I** ($5,000) - Waters multiple pots at once. Your first major purchase.\
✅ **Scarecrow** ($20,000) - Protects your crops from being destroyed by crows. Essential once you have Rare crops growing.\
✅ **Sprinkler I** ($50,000) - Automatically waters 5 pots. A massive time saver.\
✅ **Sprinkler II** ($200,000) - Covers 9 blocks.\
✅ **Sprinkler III** ($750,000) - Covers 24 pots from a single placement. A genuine server milestone.\
✅ **Greenhouse Glass** ($750,000 per block) - Lets any crop grow in any season. Lets Summer crops like Eggplant, Hops, and Pineapple run year-round. A fully greenhouse-covered farm is an endgame achievement.

**Recommended upgrade path:** Start with Watering Can I and 16 pots. Save for Sprinkler I to automate watering. Buy a Scarecrow once you have Rare crops. Aim for Sprinkler III as your long-term goal.

***

### Fertilizers

Fertilizers are consumable upgrades applied to your Planting Pot that boost your crops in different ways. There are 5 types each with 3 tiers. Apply before planting unless noted.

✅ **Quality Fertilizers** - Shift your quality roll toward Silver Star and Gold Star drops. Best used on high-value crops like Pineapple, Grape, and Red Packet Tree. Deluxe shifts 60% of drops to Silver or Gold.\
✅ **Yield Fertilizers** - Add bonus crops on top of your normal yield. Most powerful on Pepper and Eggplant. Deluxe guarantees 2 extra crops every harvest.\
✅ **Speed-Gro Fertilizers** - Cut your crop's grow time. Best on 8-stage crops like Apple Tree, Grape, and Pitaya where slow growth is the bottleneck.\
✅ **Retaining Soil Fertilizers** - Give a chance to keep soil wet between growth ticks, reducing how often you need to water. Apply any time. Great before you can afford a high-tier sprinkler.\
✅ **Magic Bottles** - Increase the chance of a rare gigantic crop variant spawning. Apply any time. Currently most effective on Cabbage and Pineapple.

***

### Tips and Good to Know

⭐ Start with Garlic. Year-round, always 4 crops, seeds self-sustain by harvest 2. No better first crop.\
⭐ Check the season before buying seeds. Nothing wastes money faster than planting Eggplant at the start of Autumn.\
⭐ Reinvest early. Every extra pot ($250) compounds your income. Aim for 16 pots, then 32, then 64 before spending on tools.\
⭐ Save hard for Sprinkler I ($50,000). Auto-watering 5 pots is a major time saver and Sprinkler III ($750,000) is the long-term dream.\
⭐ Buy a Scarecrow ($20,000) once you have Rare crops. A 1% per tick chance of losing a crop does not sound like much until a crow destroys your Pineapple plot.\
⭐ Stock up on Red Packet seeds before Winter. Seeds cost $250 each with no return so buy your supply before the season changes.\
⭐ Use Deluxe Fertilizer on your best crops. Gold Star Pineapple is $225 versus $90 normal and it pays for itself fast.\
⭐ Greenhouse Glass is the ultimate endgame goal. At $750,000 per block it is a massive investment, but letting Summer crops run year-round changes everything about your farm's income.
