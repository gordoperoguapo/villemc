# Envoy

## Envoys

Envoys are server-wide loot events where supply crates drop across the map for everyone to race toward and claim. When an envoy starts, a broadcast goes out and crates begin falling from the sky. First come, first served.

***

### How It Works

When an envoy event begins you will see a server announcement and fireworks marking where crates have landed. Run to the nearest crate and right-click it to claim your reward. Each crate gives one reward when claimed and then disappears. The event runs for 5 minutes so move fast.

Envoys run automatically every hour and a countdown warning appears at 30 minutes, 15 minutes, 10 minutes, 1 minute, and then a final countdown from 30 seconds down to 1.

***

### Crate Tiers

There are three tiers of envoy crate. Each event spawns a mix of all three. Rarer tiers spawn less frequently but have better rewards. You can tell which tier a crate is by its block type and the hologram floating above it.

#### Basic Crate

The most common crate. Appears as a **Chest**. Spawn chance: 85%.

✅ $100 Cash\
✅ 100x Claim Blocks\
✅ 1x Reward Crate Key\
✅ 0.10x vbits

***

#### Lucky Crate

Uncommon. Appears as an **Ender Chest**. Spawn chance: 25%.

✅ $500 Cash\
✅ 500x Claim Blocks\
✅ 3x Reward Crate Keys\
✅ 1x Rare Crate Key\
✅ 0.25x vbits

***

#### Titan Crate

Rare. Appears as an **Enchanting Table**. Spawn chance: 10%.

✅ $1,000 Cash\
✅ 2,000x Claim Blocks\
✅ 5x Reward Crate Keys\
✅ 1x Cosmetic Crate Key\
✅ 0.5x vbits

***

### Flares

You can trigger an envoy event yourself using a Flare. Right-click a Flare to start an envoy event at any time outside of the normal schedule. Flares can be obtained through crates and events.

***

### Tips and Good to Know

⭐ Envoys run automatically every hour so check the countdown warnings so you are ready to move.\
⭐ Crates are first come first served. Speed matters more than gear.\
⭐ The rarer the crate the better the loot. Titan crates are only a 10% spawn chance so if you see one go straight for it.\
⭐ You can spot crate tier by the block type: Chest is Basic, Ender Chest is Lucky, and Enchanting Table is Titan.\
⭐ Fireworks launch at every crate location when the event starts to help you find them fast.\
⭐ If the server is empty the event will still fire, but it waits for players to be online before announcing.
