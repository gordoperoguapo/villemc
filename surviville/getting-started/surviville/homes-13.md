# Auction House

***

## Auction House

The Auction House is a player-driven marketplace where you can buy and sell almost anything with other players. List items for a flat price, set up bidding auctions, search for what you need, and browse everything the server economy has to offer in one place. Open it with `/ah`.

***

### How It Works

There are two types of listings in the Auction House.

**Buy Now** - A flat price listing. Buyers can purchase it instantly for the listed price. No waiting, no bidding.

**Bid Auction** - A listing with a starting bid price and a bid increment. Players compete by placing bids and the highest bidder when the timer expires wins the item. When a new bid is placed the timer gets extended slightly to keep things competitive.

All listings have an expiry timer. If your item does not sell before it expires it moves to your expired items and you can reclaim it.

***

### Selling an Item

Hold the item you want to sell in your hand and run `/ah sell <price>`. This lists it as a buy now listing at the price you set.

To create a bid auction use `/ah sell <buyNowPrice> <startingBid> <bidIncrement>`. This lets buyers bid on your item or purchase it outright at the buy now price if you set one.

You can also open `/ah` and use the sell menu directly from the GUI without any commands.

***

### Buying an Item

Open `/ah` to browse all active listings. You can filter by category, sort by price, and search for specific items. Click a listing to purchase it instantly or place a bid. You cannot buy or bid on your own listings.

If you win a bid or purchase an item and your inventory is full, the item goes into your collection bin where it is held safely until you collect it.

***

### Collection Bin and Expired Items

Items you win at auction, items you buy, and listings that expire or get cancelled all land in their respective menus rather than directly in your inventory. Make sure you check these regularly so nothing is left uncollected.

✅ `/ah active` - View all your currently active listings.\
✅ `/ah expired` - View expired listings and reclaim unsold items.

***

### Searching

Use `/ah search <item name>` to search for a specific item across all active listings instead of browsing page by page.

***

### Commands

✅ `/ah` - Open the Auction House.\
✅ `/ah sell <price>` - List the item in your hand for sale at a flat price.\
✅ `/ah sell <buyNowPrice> <startingBid> <bidIncrement>` - List the item in your hand as a bid auction.\
✅ `/ah active` - View your active listings.\
✅ `/ah expired` - View your expired listings and reclaim items.\
✅ `/ah search <item>` - Search the auction house for a specific item.

***

### Tips and Good to Know

⭐ Check the Auction House before buying from the shop. Player listings often have better prices on high volume items like ores and farming goods.\
⭐ Bid auctions with a low starting price and no buy now price tend to attract more attention and competitive bidding.\
⭐ Setting a buy now price on a bid auction gives impatient buyers an option and can get your item sold faster.\
⭐ Keep an eye on your expired listings with `/ah expired` so your items do not sit uncollected.\
⭐ Use `/ah search` to quickly find a specific item instead of browsing through pages.\
⭐ You cannot bid on or buy your own listings so make sure your pricing is right before you list.
