# Player Warps

## Player Warps

Player warps let you create public teleport points that anyone on the server can visit. Whether you have a farm, a base you want to show off, or just a convenient spot you want others to be able to reach, warps make it easy to share your locations with the community.

***

### Getting Started

Every player starts with **1 warp slot**. You can unlock more by ranking up. To set a warp, stand at the location you want to share and type `/pw set <name>`. Once it is set, other players can teleport to it using `/pw <name>`.

***

### Commands

#### Basic Commands

✅ `/pw <warp>` - Teleport to a player warp.\
✅ `/pw set <name>` - Set a warp at your current location.\
✅ `/pw remove <warp>` - Remove one of your warps.\
✅ `/pw reset <warp>` - Move an existing warp to your current location.\
✅ `/pw rename <warp> <newname>` - Rename one of your warps.\
✅ `/pw list` - Browse all public warps on the server.\
✅ `/pw search <query>` - Search for a warp or player.\
✅ `/pw near` - See warps that are nearby your current location.\
✅ `/pw rtp` - Teleport to a random warp.\
✅ `/pw info <warp>` - View information about a specific warp.\
✅ `/pw amount` - Check how many warps you have set.\
✅ `/pw open` - Open the warp browser menu.\
✅ `/pw favourite <warp>` - Add a warp to your favourites list.

#### Managing Your Warp

✅ `/pw desc set <warp> <description>` - Add a description to your warp so visitors know what it is.\
✅ `/pw desc remove <warp>` - Remove your warp description.\
✅ `/pw icon set <warp>` - Set a custom icon for your warp in the menu.\
✅ `/pw icon remove <warp>` - Remove your warp icon.\
✅ `/pw category set <warp> <category>` - Assign your warp to a category.\
✅ `/pw lock <warp>` - Lock your warp so it is hidden from the public list and only you can teleport to it.\
✅ `/pw settings <warp>` - Open the warp settings menu.

#### Visitor Controls

✅ `/pw whitelist set <warp> <player>` - Restrict your warp to specific players only.\
✅ `/pw whitelist remove <warp> <player>` - Remove a player from your whitelist.\
✅ `/pw whitelist list <warp>` - View your current whitelist.\
✅ `/pw ban set <warp> <player>` - Ban a player from your warp.\
✅ `/pw ban remove <warp> <player>` - Unban a player from your warp.\
✅ `/pw managers set <warp> <player>` - Add a manager who can help manage your warp.\
✅ `/pw managers remove <warp> <player>` - Remove a manager from your warp.\
✅ `/pw rate <warp> <rating>` - Rate a warp you have visited.\
✅ `/pw setowner <warp> <player>` - Transfer ownership of your warp to another player.

***

### Warp Limits by Rank

The number of warps you can set depends on your rank.

⭐ **Default** - 1 warp\
⭐ **Mortal** - 2 warps\
⭐ **Wanderer** - 3 warps\
⭐ **Rogue** - 4 warps\
⭐ **Sovereign** - 5 warps\
⭐ **Radiant** - 5 warps\
⭐ **Mythic** - 10 warps\
⭐ **Eternal** - 20 warps

***

### Tips and Good to Know

⭐ Adding a description and category to your warp makes it much easier for other players to find and understand what your warp is for.\
⭐ You can lock your warp at any time to hide it from the public list without deleting it.\
⭐ Whitelisting your warp means only the players you approve can teleport to it.\
⭐ Players can rate your warp, so the better your build the more attention it will get.\
⭐ You can use `/pw near` to discover warps around you while exploring the world.\
⭐ Warps that are inactive for a long time may be purged automatically, so make sure you log in regularly to keep yours active.
