# Spawners

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (32).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>



Spawners are one of the best passive income sources on Surviville. Place one down and it quietly generates loot and XP over time - no babysitting required. Stack them, upgrade them, and collect everything with a single click.

***

### Getting Started

Spawners can be purchased from the Shop using `/shop`. Once you have one, place it down anywhere in your claim. It will immediately start generating loot on its own timer, storing everything inside until you come to collect.

You can place up to **5 spawners** by default with ranks offering higher amounts. Each spawner is a separate placement - stacking multiple of the same type onto one block does not count against your limit.

> 💡 Spawners only generate loot when you are online. They do not produce anything while you're logged off, so there's no need to worry about coming back to an overflowing storage.

***

### Spawner Types

The following spawners are available in the shop. Rarer spawners cost more but generate more valuable loot.

| Spawner  | Shop Price |
| -------- | ---------- |
| Zombie   | $1,000     |
| Skeleton | $1,500     |
| Creeper  | $2,500     |
| Witch    | $10,000    |
| Guardian | $50,000    |

Additional spawner types exist and may be obtainable through other means such as crates or events.

***

### Upgrades & Levels

Every spawner has **10 upgrade levels**. Higher levels mean faster generation speed, more loot slots, and more XP storage. Upgrading is done directly through the spawner GUI by clicking on the spawner after placing it.

The cost to upgrade scales with the spawner's level - early upgrades are cheap, but higher levels get significantly more expensive. The formula used is `level ^ 5 × 1000`, so plan accordingly before dumping everything into one spawner.

Here's what changes as you level up a spawner:

| Level | Speed  | Loot Slots | XP Storage |
| ----- | ------ | ---------- | ---------- |
| 1     | 50-60s | 3          | 150        |
| 2     | 45-55s | 9          | 300        |
| 3     | 40-50s | 18         | 650        |
| 4     | 35-45s | 27         | 1,000      |
| 5     | 30-40s | 45         | 1,500      |
| 6     | 25-35s | 63         | 2,100      |
| 7     | 20-25s | 81         | 3,000      |
| 8     | 15-25s | 108        | 4,500      |
| 9     | 10-20s | 135        | 7,000      |
| 10    | 5-10s  | 180        | 10,000     |



***

### Collecting Loot & XP

When you click on a placed spawner, you'll open its GUI. From there you can:

* View the current loot stored inside and sell it directly
* Withdraw stored XP
* Upgrade the spawner to the next level
* Stack additional spawners of the same type onto it

**If you have the perk** you can also right-click on the spawner in your `/myspawners` list to instantly sell all loot and withdraw XP without opening the full GUI.

***

### Stacking

Multiple spawners of the same type can be stacked together into a single block. Stacking increases how much loot is generated per cycle, making your setup more compact and powerful. The default stack limit is **20 spawners per block**.

To add to an existing stack, simply right-click the placed spawner while holding a spawner of the same type.

***

### Boosters

Boosters are temporary multipliers that supercharge your spawners for a limited time. Three types are available in the game:

⭐ **Loot Booster** - Multiplies the amount of loot generated per cycle.\
⭐ **Speed Booster** - Multiplies how fast the spawner generates loot (lower cycle time).\
⭐ **XP Booster** - Multiplies the XP generated per cycle.

To activate a booster, right-click on a placed spawner while holding it. Boosters apply to all spawner types and last for a set duration shown on the item.

***

### Wands

Wands let you interact with multiple spawners at once in a radius around you, saving you from clicking each one individually.

⭐ **Sell Wand** - Right-click to instantly sell loot from all nearby spawners within its radius.\
⭐ **XP Wand** - Right-click to instantly withdraw XP from all nearby spawners within its radius.

Both wands have a limited number of uses displayed on the item, and work on all spawner types.

***

### Tips & Good to Know

⭐ Spawners are protected from explosions, pistons, and other block-destroying mechanics - they won't be lost to a creeper or a griefer.\
⭐ When you break a spawner, any loot stored inside is automatically sold rather than dropped - you won't lose your items.\
⭐ Spawners must be placed inside a claimed area to keep them protected. Anyone who can build at the spawner's location can interact with it.\
⭐ Stacking is the most efficient way to scale up - a maxed-out stack of 20 spawners at level 10 generates dramatically more than 20 individual level 1 spawners.
