# vbits

## vbits

vbits are VilleMC's premium currency. They sit alongside regular money and are used for exclusive purchases and upgrades that money alone cannot buy. They are rarer than standard currency but there are multiple ways to earn them just by playing.

***

### What Are vbits Used For?

vbits are accepted in the rank store and various premium menus across VilleMC. They represent a higher tier of value than regular money and are tied to some of the best rewards and upgrades available across the network. Because vbits are shared across all servers, anything you earn on Surviville, Skyville, or Arcadeville counts toward the same balance.

***

### How to Get vbits

#### Buy from the Store

The most direct way. Purchase vbits from the online store at **store.villemc.net** for $1.00 per vbit. Buying supports the server and gives you instant access to the currency.

#### Earn In-Game

vbits can be earned through regular gameplay across several systems on any VilleMC server. None of these require spending real money.

⭐ **Battle Pass (Free Track)** - The free pass pays out small vbit amounts at select tiers throughout the season.\
⭐ **Battle Pass (Premium Track)** - The premium pass pays out 0.25 vbits at nearly every single tier, making it one of the best ongoing sources of vbits in the game. A full season delivers 15+ vbits just from tier payouts alone.\
⭐ **Crates** - Various crate rewards include vbit payouts. Rare and Ultra Rare crates both have vbit rewards.\
⭐ **Envoys** - All three envoy tiers pay out vbits when collected. Titan envoys give the most.\
⭐ **Seasonal Content** - Limited time events and seasonal rewards may include vbit payouts.

### Tips and Good to Know

⭐ vbits are shared across all VilleMC servers so your balance follows you between Surviville, Skyville, and Arcadeville.\
⭐ The Premium Battle Pass is the single best source of vbits for active players. Completing a full season through normal gameplay earns a significant amount.\
⭐ Even the free Battle Pass track pays out vbits at certain milestones so you earn some just by playing.\
⭐ Collecting every envoy consistently adds up over time. Prioritize Titan envoys when they spawn for the best vbit yield.\
⭐ vbits do not expire so you can save them up over multiple seasons before spending.
