# Crates

## Crates

Crates are a way to earn exclusive rewards ranging from in-game currency and useful items to cosmetics, special gear, and keys to even better crates. Keys can be found through voting, events, competitions, and the store.

***

### How It Works

Each crate requires its matching key to open. Walk up to a crate at spawn and right-click it with the key in your hand. A spinning preview animation plays showing the possible rewards before landing on your result. You can also preview what a crate contains before opening by right-clicking without a key.\

#### Cosmetic Crate

Packed with purely cosmetic rewards to customize your appearance and base. None of these affect gameplay which means everything here is for style points only.

**Player Glows** - White, Black, Dark Blue, Blue, Dark Aqua, Aqua, Dark Gray, Gray, Dark Red, Red, Gold, Pink, Green, Dark Green, and Yellow. Right-click to redeem and apply your glow color.

**Disguises** - Dog, Axolotl, Panda, Creeper, Warden, and Phantom disguises. Right-click to apply.

**Plushies** - Decorative custom plushie items including Creaking, Zombie Horse, Tropical Fish, Steve, Ocelot, Alex, Camel, Cat, Dolphin, Frog, Horse, Mooshroom, Panda, Rabbit, and Wolf.

***

#### Rune Crate (Monthly)

The rarest and most prestigious crate on the server. Each month features a brand new themed set of exclusive custom items that cannot be obtained anywhere else. The current theme, loot pool, and gear set rotate every month so each edition is unique and time limited.

Rune Crate rewards are powerful custom items, exclusive gear sets, and high-value economy payouts. Once the month ends the current set is gone for good.

> 💡 The Rune Crate theme changes monthly. Stock up on keys before the rotation if you are chasing the current set.

***

### Tips and Good to Know

⭐ You can preview any crate's full loot pool by right-clicking the crate without a key.\
⭐ Reward Crate keys can drop keys for every higher tier crate so opening them consistently is always worth it.\
⭐ Cosmetic rewards are purely visual and do not give any gameplay advantage.\
⭐ Monthly Crate loot is time limited. Once the month ends the current set is gone for good.\
⭐ Keys can be earned through voting, weekend events, competitions, in-game contests, and the store at store.surviville.org.
