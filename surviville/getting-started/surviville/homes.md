# Homes

## Homes

Homes let you save locations around the world and teleport back to them whenever you want. No more getting lost or running back across the map every time you log in.

***

### Getting Started

Every player starts with **1 home slot**. You can increase this by ranking up. Check the Ranks page for a full breakdown of how many homes each rank unlocks.

To set a home, simply stand where you want to save it and type `/sethome <name>`. You can name it whatever you like so it is easy to remember.

***

### Commands

✅ `/sethome <name>` - Save your current location as a home with a custom name.\
✅ `/home <name>` - Teleport to one of your saved homes.\
✅ `/delhome <name>` - Delete a saved home.\
✅ `/homes` - List all of your currently saved homes.

***

### Home Limits by Rank

The number of homes you can set depends on your rank.

⭐ **Default** - 1 home\
⭐ **Mortal** - 2 home\
⭐ **Wanderer** - 3 home\
⭐ **Rogue** - 5 home\
⭐ **Sovereign** - 8 homes\
⭐ **Radiant** - 15 homes\
⭐ **Mythic** - 30 homes\
⭐ **Eternal** - Unlimited homes

***

### Tips and Good to Know

⭐ Home names are case sensitive, so `base` and `Base` are treated as two different homes.\
⭐ You can overwrite an existing home by running `/sethome` with the same name again.\
⭐ Homes can be set anywhere in the world including underground, inside buildings, and in the Nether.\
⭐ If your rank is downgraded and you have more homes than your new limit allows, your existing homes will remain but you will not be able to set new ones until you are back under the limit.
