# Orders

## Orders

The order system is a player-driven marketplace where you can request items you need and pay other players to deliver them. It cuts out the middleman and lets the server economy run itself. If you need something, post an order and let someone else bring it to you. If you have surplus goods, browse open orders and get paid instantly for delivering them.

***

### How It Works for Buyers

1. Open the order menu with `/order`.
2. Click New Order and select the item you want to buy.
3. Type the quantity you need in chat.
4. Type the price per unit you are willing to pay in chat. The full cost is reserved from your balance immediately so you can not overspend.
5. Your order goes live in the public marketplace for any player to see and fulfill.
6. As players deliver items to your order you will be notified. Delivered items go into your private stash.
7. Open `/order` and collect from your stash whenever you like. Items in your stash are safe and saved until you take them.

***

### How It Works for Sellers

1. Open `/order` to browse the public marketplace.
2. Filter or sort orders to find ones you can fulfill. You can sort by highest price, most recent, or most delivered, and filter by category.
3. Click an order you want to fulfill and deliver your items into the confirmation GUI.
4. You do not need to fill the whole order at once. Partial deliveries are fully supported so deliver whatever you have and get paid for that portion immediately.
5. If you have a Shulker Box full of items you can deliver the whole box at once. The system automatically unpacks it, takes what the order needs, and returns anything extra to your inventory.

***

### Orders and Stash

When a buyer's order is partially or fully filled, the delivered items land in their private stash rather than their inventory. This means buyers do not need to be online to receive deliveries. Open your stash from the `/order` menu to collect your items at any time. Items stay in the stash until you take them.

***

### Order Expiry

All orders have an expiry timer shown on the order itself. If your order expires before it is fully fulfilled, any remaining reserved funds are automatically refunded to your balance and any items already delivered stay in your stash ready to collect.

***

### Commands

✅ `/order` - Open the order menu to browse the marketplace, create orders, manage your active orders, and access your stash.

***

### Tips and Good to Know

⭐ Funds are reserved the moment you create an order so make sure you have enough coins before posting.\
⭐ You do not need to be online to receive deliveries. Items go straight to your stash and wait for you.\
⭐ Partial deliveries are allowed so sellers can fulfill whatever portion they have. You do not need to wait for one player to fill the whole order.\
⭐ Shulker Boxes can be used to bulk deliver items to an order in one action, which is great for large quantity orders.\
⭐ Sort by Highest Price when browsing as a seller to find the most profitable orders first.\
⭐ Keep an eye on your order expiry timers. If an order expires any remaining funds are returned to you automatically.
