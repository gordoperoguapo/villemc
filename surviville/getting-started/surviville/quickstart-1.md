# Kits

## Kits

Kits give you a head start with gear, tools, and supplies. Open the kit menu with `/kit` to browse everything available to you. Some kits are available to all players, others are tied to your rank. Each kit has a cooldown so you can claim it again once the timer resets.\
<br>

### Commands

✅ `/kit` - Open the kit menu to browse and claim all available kits.\
✅ `/kit <name>` - Claim a specific kit directly by name.

***

### Tips and Good to Know

⭐ Right-click any kit in the menu to preview its contents before claiming.\
⭐ Kits respect your inventory space so make sure you have room before claiming a large kit.\
⭐ Each kit has its own independent cooldown so you can claim multiple kits at the same time if you are eligible for them.\
⭐ The Player and Claiming kits are available to everyone regardless of rank so claim them regularly.\
⭐ Higher rank kits like Eternal have longer cooldowns but the quality of the gear makes them well worth waiting for.<br>
