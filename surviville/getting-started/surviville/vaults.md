# Player Vaults

## Player Vaults

Player vaults are personal storage chests you can access from anywhere in the world. No need to run back to your base to grab something. Just open your vault and it is right there.

***

### Getting Started

Every player starts with **1 vault** by default. You can unlock more by ranking up. Check the Ranks page for a full breakdown of how many vaults each rank includes.

To open your vault, simply type `/pv` followed by the vault number you want to open. If you only have one vault, just type `/pv 1`.

***

### Commands

✅ `/pv` - Open the vault selector menu to browse all of your vaults.\
✅ `/pv <number>` - Open a specific vault directly by number.

***

### Vault Limits by Rank

The number of vaults you can access depends on your rank.

⭐ **Default** - 1 vault\
⭐ **Mortal** - 3 vault\
⭐ **Wanderer** - 5 vault\
⭐ **Rogue** - 8 vault\
⭐ **Sovereign** - 10 vaults\
⭐ **Radiant** - 15 vaults\
⭐ **Mythic** - 35 vaults\
⭐ **Eternal** - 50 vaults

***

### Tips and Good to Know

⭐ Vaults are accessible from anywhere, including the Nether and the End.\
⭐ Custom items, crate keys, and other special items can be stored in vaults safely.\
⭐ Your vault contents are tied to your account and will be there every time you log in.\
⭐ You can customize the icon for each vault in the selector menu to help keep things organized.\
⭐ Vaults are personal storage only. Other players cannot access your vaults.
