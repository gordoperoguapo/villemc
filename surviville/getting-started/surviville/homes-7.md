# Spawners

## Spawners

Spawners are automated loot generators you can place anywhere in your claim. Once placed they work passively and fill up with items and XP that you collect whenever you like. No babysitting required.\
<br>

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (31).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

***

### How It Works

Place a spawner inside your claim and it will begin generating loot automatically on a timer. Loot and XP are stored inside the spawner until you collect them. Left-click the spawner to open its inventory and collect manually, or right-click to instantly sell all loot and withdraw all XP at once.

Each spawner displays a hologram above it showing its current level, storage fill percentage, and time until the next spawn cycle.

***

### Upgrading Spawners

Every spawner starts at Level 1 and can be upgraded up to Level 10. Upgrading increases generation speed, loot storage slots, XP storage, and the loot multiplier. Higher levels generate loot significantly faster with much larger multipliers.

Upgrade cost is calculated per level using the formula: Level^5 x 1,000. This means upgrades get increasingly expensive at higher levels so plan your investments carefully.

### Wands

Wands let you interact with multiple spawners at once from a distance.

✅ **Sell Wand** - Right-click to instantly sell all loot from nearby spawners in a radius. Shows total items sold and money earned.\
✅ **XP Wand** - Right-click to instantly withdraw all stored XP from nearby spawners in a radius.

Both wands have a limited number of uses and work on all spawner types.

***

### Boosters

Boosters are consumable items applied directly to a spawner by right-clicking while holding them. Each booster has a multiplier and a duration.

✅ **Speed Booster** - Increases how fast the spawner generates loot for a set duration.\
✅ **Loot Booster** - Multiplies the amount of loot the spawner produces.\
✅ **XP Booster** - Multiplies the XP generated by the spawner.

All three boosters work on every spawner type.

***

### Commands

✅ `/myspawners` - View all of your placed spawners and their current status.

***

### Tips and Good to Know

⭐ Spawners only generate loot while the owner is online. They pause when you are offline.\
⭐ Right-click a spawner to sell and collect in one action instead of opening the GUI.\
⭐ Upgrading to higher levels dramatically increases output. A Level 10 spawner produces 5x the loot of a Level 1 at nearly twice the speed.\
⭐ You can stack multiple spawners of the same type in one block to multiply output. The stack limit is 20 by default.\
⭐ You can place up to 5 different spawner types by default. This limit can be increased with rank permissions.\
⭐ Sell Wands and XP Wands are great for players with large spawner setups since they collect from multiple spawners in one right-click.\
⭐ Silk Touch is not required to break and move spawners on Surviville.
