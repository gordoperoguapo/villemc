# Making Money

#### Early Game

You just spawned in with empty pockets. Your goal right now is to stack small, reliable income sources on top of each other. Nothing in this section requires any real upfront investment.

**Pick Your Job**

Open `/jobs` and join a job. You can only hold one job at a time, so choose the one that matches how you actually play. Miner, Farmer, Woodcutter, and Hunter are all strong early picks since they pay you automatically for things you were going to do anyway. Your job levels up to 200 as you work it, and higher levels mean better payouts, so it pays to commit to one rather than hopping around. You also earn job points alongside your money, which you can spend on exclusive rewards in `/jobs shop`.

**Run the Envoys**

Envoys are timed loot events that run every hour. When one starts, use `/envoy` to head to the envoy arena, where supply crates spawn for everyone to race toward. Crates are first come, first served and each one pays out money, claim blocks, crate keys, and even vbits. There are three tiers, and the rarer the crate the better the haul.

⭐ **Basic Crate** (Chest) - $100, claim blocks, a Reward Key, and a small vbits payout.\
⭐ **Lucky Crate** (Ender Chest) - $500, more claim blocks, multiple keys including a Rare Key, and more vbits.\
⭐ **Titan Crate** (Enchanting Table) - $1,000, a big claim block payout, five Reward Keys, a Cosmetic Key, and the largest vbits payout.

An active envoy run can multiply an early game balance several times over in five minutes. Never skip one. Watch for the countdown warnings in chat so you are ready when it starts.

**Vote Every Day**

Voting is the best money-per-minute activity on the server and it scales with nothing. Use `/vote` to see all the sites. Every vote pays out rewards and earns you vote coins, which you can spend in the vote shop on straight cash, crate keys, and even vbits. An active voter hitting every site daily builds a serious side income for a couple minutes of effort.

**Work the Battle Pass**

Open `/battlepass` and start knocking out quests. Daily quests refresh every day at noon and weekly quests rotate through the season, covering things like mining, farming, fishing, and hunting that you are already doing. Tier rewards on the free track include cash payouts up to $8,000, crate keys, minerals, and vbits. The Battle Pass is one of the few systems that pays out real money milestones just for playing normally, so claim your rewards regularly.

**Claim Your Playtime Rewards**

Check `/rewards` as you play. Playtime milestones award crate keys just for being on the server. The keys themselves are money too, since crates pay out cash, claim blocks, and keys to even better crates. On top of that, everyone online receives a small automatic money payout every couple of minutes, so time on the server is never wasted.

**Hunt Mobs and Wildlife**

Regular mob kills pay money on top of any Hunter job earnings. But the real early game secret is the custom wildlife roaming the world. Killing wildlife with a bow as the finishing blow awards crate keys, with a chance at Rare and even Ultra Rare keys. Check `/hunting` for the full wildlife guide. A bow, some arrows, and a walk through the wilderness is a legitimate income strategy from day one.

**Start a Small Farm**

Buy a few Planting Pots ($250 each) and cheap seeds from `/shop` under Farming. Seeds are investments, so expect a few harvests before you break even, then everything after that is pure profit. Garlic grows year round and is the perfect first crop while you learn the seasons. Check `/farming` for the full guide.

**Open Your Crates**

All those keys you are collecting from envoys, votes, playtime, hunting, and the Battle Pass turn into money at the crates at spawn. Crates pay out cash, claim blocks, vbits, and keys to higher tier crates. Reward Keys in particular can chain upward into Rare, Ultra Rare, and Cosmetic keys, so opening them consistently is always worth it.

⭐ **Early game goal:** A job you are leveling, a starter garlic farm, and a habit of never missing a vote, an envoy, or a daily quest. This combination should carry you comfortably into your first big purchases.

#### Mid Game

You have a base, a farm, and money saved. Now it is time to convert that money into systems that earn more money, and to start selling to other players instead of only the server.

**Scale Your Farm**

Reinvest your farming profits into more pots and higher tier seeds. Move up from common crops into uncommon and rare ones, and start buying fertilizers to boost yield, speed, and quality. Quality matters a lot, since Silver Star and Gold Star crops sell for 1.5x and 2.5x the normal price. Learn the season calendar and plant with the rotation instead of against it. A well planned multi-season farm is the most reliable income engine in the mid game.

**Get Serious About Fishing**

Fishing is a full custom economy of its own. Every fish you catch has a sell value, and rarer catches at higher quality are worth serious money. Sell your haul with `/sellfish` whenever your bag fills up. The rod ladder is steep, starting around $500 for a beginner rod and climbing to $75,000 at the top, and every upgrade unlocks better and more valuable catches. Buy the best rod you can afford and reinvest your fishing profits into the next one. Check `/fishing` for the full guide.

**Learn to Cook**

The cooking system turns two cheap ingredients into dishes that sell for far more than their parts. Fish dishes sit at the top of the recipe list, which pairs perfectly with your fishing habit since you can choose between selling a catch raw or cooking it up for a bigger payout. Check the cooking guide for every recipe and its sell price.

**Buy Your First Spawner**

Spawners are the biggest money machines on Surviville, and this is the stage to enter. Spawners generate drops into their storage automatically, and selling that storage is steady income that accumulates whether you are grinding or not. Cheaper shop spawners pay themselves off within a few days of activity and then print profit forever. Buy the best one you can afford, keep the storage sold, and reinvest into upgrades or additional spawners. Spawner types not sold in the shop come from the Ultra Rare Crate's random spawner voucher.

**Fill Orders**

Orders are requests posted by other players who need specific items and are willing to pay for them. Check the Orders menu regularly, because players often pay above shop price for convenience. If your farm, spawners, or mining trips produce a surplus, filling orders is free extra margin on items you were producing anyway.

**Sell on the Auction House**

The Auction House with `/ah` is where you sell to other players. List items at a flat buy-now price or set up bidding auctions for rare finds. Crate loot, rare fish, high quality crops, enchanted gear, and custom items all move well here, and player demand often beats shop prices. Before dumping anything valuable into `/shop`, check what it goes for on the Auction House first.

**Open Your Own Shop**

Ready to become a merchant? You can create your own chest shops and sell directly to other players at prices you set. Stock what the server actually needs, like building blocks, food, crop produce, and mob drops, then set up a player warp so customers can find you. A well stocked shop in a good location earns money while you are off doing literally anything else.

**Fight the World Boss**

Powerful bosses spawn three times a day, and everyone who participates earns rewards. Payouts are split across damage, tank, and support roles, so you do not need the best gear on the server to walk away with loot. Check `/raid` for info and `/raidtop` to see where you rank against other hunters.

⭐ **Mid game goal:** An upgraded spawner or two, a serious multi-season farm, a mid tier rod, and your first sales to actual players. Your money should now grow even on days you barely log in.

#### Late Game

You have capital. The late game is about compounding it and chasing the highest value content on the server.

**Build a Spawner Empire**

Stack, upgrade, and expand your spawners up to your placement limit. Upgraded spawners produce dramatically more per cycle, and boosters multiply your payouts on top of that. A maxed out spawner network is the largest passive income source in the game. Higher ranks raise your spawner limits and Radiant and above can mine and relocate spawners with Silk Touch, so ranking up directly raises your ceiling. Hunt the Ultra Rare Crate for random spawner vouchers to collect the types money cannot buy.

**Master Tier Farming**

Move into the exotic crops. Dragon Fruit and the Red Packet Tree are the most valuable crops on the server, with the Red Packet Tree ruling the Winter season when most farms go quiet. Greenhouse Glass ($750,000) removes seasonal restrictions entirely so your best crops never stop, and top tier sprinklers and Deluxe fertilizers automate and amplify everything. At Gold Star quality with maxed infrastructure, a late game farm is a fortune on its own.

**Fish the Deep End**

With a top tier rod you unlock the rarest and most valuable catches, including fish that can only be pulled from lava. Between rare fish sales, high quality multipliers, and cooking your catches into the top dishes, a dedicated fishing session at this level outearns almost any early game grind. Fishing competitions add prizes on top for the best anglers.

**Own the Boss Fights**

With late game gear you can consistently top the damage boards at all three daily boss spawns. Regulars earn money, keys, and loot that is not available anywhere else, and `/raidtop` bragging rights come free with it.

**Play the Market Like a Trader**

At this stage the player market becomes an income source of its own. Buy underpriced Auction House listings and relist them at their real value. Corner the market on items in demand, flip rare crate loot, and watch what the Rune Crate is offering each month since that loot rotates out forever and only becomes rarer. The richest players on the server make as much from trading as they do from grinding.

**Convert Everything**

Late game wealth is about efficiency. Surplus crate keys, spare cosmetics, extra claim blocks, duplicate rare items, all of it can be turned into money through the Auction House, Orders, or your shop. If it is sitting in a vault doing nothing, it is losing you money.

⭐ **Late game goal:** Multiple income streams running at once. Spawners and farms earning passively, fishing and boss fights when you are online, and the market working your money while you sleep.

***

#### Tips and Good to Know

⭐ Stack passive and active income. Your farm grows and your spawner storage fills while you fish, hunt envoys, or fight the boss.\
⭐ Never skip voting or the daily Battle Pass quests. Both cost minutes and pay out every single day.\
⭐ Keys are money. Every key from votes, envoys, playtime, hunting, and the Battle Pass eventually turns into cash, claim blocks, or items you can sell.\
⭐ Check player prices before shop prices. The Auction House and Orders often pay more than `/shop` for the same item.\
⭐ Seeds, rods, and spawners are investments. Judge them by long term profit, not first day returns.\
⭐ Watch the seasons. Planting a crop right before its season ends is wasted money.\
⭐ Quality beats quantity in farming and fishing. Higher star crops and rarer catches are worth multiples of the common stuff.\
⭐ Envoys, votes, crates, and the Battle Pass all pay out vbits, so active players earn premium currency without spending a dime.<br>
