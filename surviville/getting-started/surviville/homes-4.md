# Enchants

## Custom Enchantments

Surviville has a full custom enchantment system with over 180 unique enchantments across six rarity tiers. These go far beyond vanilla enchantments and can be applied to weapons, tools, armor, bows, fishing rods, and more.\

***

### How It Works

Custom enchantment books can be obtained from crates, the Battle Pass, and envoys. Right-click a book to open it and receive a random enchantment from that tier, then drag the resulting book onto an item to apply it. Each item has a limited number of enchantment slots so choose carefully.

Books are color coded by tier so you always know what you are working with at a glance.

***

### Enchantment Tiers

Enchantments are sorted into six tiers from common to incredibly rare. Higher tiers contain more powerful and more unusual effects.

⬜ **Simple** - The most common tier. Straightforward bonuses and effects for all item types.\
🟩 **Unique** - A step up with more interesting effects including mob-specific damage, hunger management, and crop utilities.\
🟦 **Elite** - Strong combat and utility enchantments with more complex effects like area damage, special arrow behaviors, and defensive procs.\
🟨 **Ultimate** - Powerful enchantments with significant gameplay impact including 3x3 mining, AoE combat, advanced fishing, and major defensive abilities.\
🟧 **Legendary** - Rare and impactful enchantments including lifesteal, lava walking, water walking, unlocking extra hearts, and unbreakable weapons.\
🟪 **Fabled** - The rarest tier. Soul-powered enchantments, phoenix revival, soulbound keep-on-death, item duplication, and more.

***

### Applying Enchantments

1. Get an enchantment book from a crate, envoy, or the Battle Pass.
2. Right-click it to reveal the specific enchantment inside.
3. Drag the book onto your item in your inventory to apply it.
4. Each application has a success and destroy chance shown on the book. Higher tier enchantments are harder to apply.
5. If an application fails the book is consumed. If it destroys, both the book and enchantment slot may be lost.

Items can hold up to 9 enchantment slots by default, expandable to 13 with Slot Increasers.

***

### Useful Items

**Magic Dust** - Boosts the success chance of applying an enchantment book. Use before dragging a book onto your item.

**Mystery Dust** - A lower quality byproduct of magic dust. Less effective but still useful.

**Transmog Scroll** - Reorganizes the enchantments on an item by rarity and adds a lore count to the name. Drag onto any item to apply.

**Soul Gem** - Stores souls. Used to power certain Fabled enchantments that consume souls per activation.

**Soul Tracker** - Apply to a weapon to begin collecting souls from kills. Required for soul-powered Fabled enchantments.

**Slot Increaser** - Adds additional enchantment slots to an item so you can apply more enchantments. Items start with a base slot limit and can be increased up to a maximum of 13.

### Enchantment List Highlights

With over 180 enchantments available there is far too much to list everything here. Here are some standouts by category.

**Farming and Mining** - Harvest (3x3 hoe), Vein Miner, Trench (3x3 dig), Timber (one-hit trees), Replanter, Planter, Gemify (ores drop blocks), Telepathy (auto-pickup drops).

**Fishing** - Auto Reel, Lucky, Bait (double drops), Poisoned Hook, Sharpness Hook.

**Weapons** - Lifesteal, Killaura, Doublestrike, Disarm, Phoenix (revive on death), Soulbound (keep on death), Nulify, Diploid (multiply mob drops).

**Armor** - Overload (extra hearts), Wings (fly while worn), Lava Walker, Water Walker, Immortal (armor durability protection), Dodge, Angelic (heal on hit), Disappear (go invisible at low HP).

**Bows** - Striker (rain arrows), Sniper (headshot double damage), Bowmaster, Hellfire (fireballs), Longbow.

**Elytra** - Momentum, Rush, Slingshot, Reinforced, Judgement.

For the full list of every enchantment and what it does, visit the complete enchantment reference here: [https://ae.advancedplugins.net/enchantments/list-of-enchantments/default-enchants-200+](https://ae.advancedplugins.net/enchantments/list-of-enchantments/default-enchants-200+)

You can also use `/ae info <enchantment>` in game to look up any specific enchantment.

### Tips and Good to Know

⭐ Items have a slot limit. Plan your enchantment loadout before spending books on an item you might replace.\
⭐ Fabled enchantments marked with soul costs require a Soul Tracker on your weapon and an active soul supply to activate.\
⭐ Use Magic Dust before applying high tier books to increase your success chance.\
⭐ The Transmog Scroll is purely cosmetic and organizes your enchantment lore without changing the enchantments themselves.\
⭐ Combining two books of the same enchantment and level on an anvil upgrades it to the next level if a higher level exists.\
⭐ Right-click enchantment books from crates before using them so you know what enchantment is inside.
