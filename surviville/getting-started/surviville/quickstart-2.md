# AFK

## AFK Rewards

Surviville has a dedicated AFK world where you can earn rewards just for being present. Head to the AFK world, stay put, and collect a payout every 2 minutes automatically. It is a great way to earn passively while you are tabbed out or taking a break.\
<br>

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (26).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

***

### How It Works

Use `/afk` to travel to the AFK world. After 2 minutes of AFK time your reward is paid out automatically and the timer resets. You will see a subtitle notification when you enter, when you leave, and when a reward is paid out so you always know what is happening.

If you leave the AFK world your progress resets and the timer starts over when you return.

***

### Rewards

Every 2 minutes in the AFK world you receive $25 automatically.

If you have the Premium AFK perk you receive an upgraded reward instead. Each payout rolls one of two possible outcomes:

⭐ **99% chance** - $100 cash\
⭐ **1% chance** - 0.25 vbits

The 1% vbit payout is a lucky bonus on top of an already solid passive income rate. Premium AFK payouts also trigger a firework effect so the whole server sees when you get lucky.

***

### Commands

✅ `/afk` - Travel to the AFK world.

***

### Tips and Good to Know

⭐ Your AFK timer resets if you leave the AFK world so stay put once you are in.\
⭐ At the base rate of $25 every 2 minutes that works out to $750 per hour just for being present.\
⭐ Premium AFK earns $100 every 2 minutes which is $3,000 per hour plus the chance at vbits on top.\
⭐ The 1% vbit payout is rare but adds up over long AFK sessions.\
⭐ You will see a subtitle showing your progress toward the next reward so you always know how close you are.<br>
