# Battlepass

## Battle Pass

The Battle Pass is a seasonal progression system that rewards you for playing normally. Complete quests, earn points, level up through tiers, and claim rewards along the way. The more you play, the more you earn.

***

### How It Works

The Battle Pass has two tracks running side by side: a free track and a premium track. Everyone progresses through both tracks at the same time by completing quests. The difference is which rewards you can actually claim at each tier.

Every quest you complete gives you points. Points fill your tier progress bar. When the bar fills you level up to the next tier and can claim whatever reward is waiting there. Rewards are not claimed automatically so make sure to open your Battle Pass menu and collect them.

Open your Battle Pass with /battlepass.

***

### Free vs Premium

Everyone gets the free pass for no cost. The free track has rewards spread across all 60 tiers and is a great way to earn crate keys, minerals, currency, and more just by playing.

The Premium Pass unlocks the premium reward track which runs alongside the free track. Premium players claim rewards at almost every single tier and get significantly more vbits across the entire season, plus milestone bonuses and a Rune Crate Key at tier 60. The Premium Pass is available on the store at store.surviville.org.

Premium players still receive all free track rewards on top of their premium rewards.

***

### Quests

Quests are how you earn points and progress through the pass. There are two types.

**Weekly Quests** refresh each week and are split between free and premium tracks. Each week brings a new set of objectives drawn from a rotating pool of activities like mining, farming, fishing, hunting, crafting, and more. There are 4 weeks of quests per season.

**Daily Quests** refresh every day at noon. Nine daily quests are available at a time and cover a wide variety of quick tasks. Completing these consistently is the fastest way to keep leveling through the season.

When you complete a quest you get a level up sound and title notification. A boss bar tracks your progress toward the next tier in real time.

***

### Free Pass Rewards

The free track spans 60 tiers with rewards at specific milestones. Here is what you can earn across the season:

**Crate Keys** - Reward Keys, Rare Keys, Ultra Rare Keys, and Cosmetic Keys scattered throughout the tiers.\
**In-Game Currency** - Cash payouts ranging from $1,000 to $8,000 at various milestones.\
**vbits** - Small vbits payouts at select tiers throughout the pass.\
**Minerals** - Coal, Iron Ingots, Lapis Lazuli, Redstone, Gold Ingots, Diamonds, and Emeralds.\
**Enchanted Tools** - Enchanted diamond tools and weapons including pickaxes, axes, swords, shovels, and hoes.\
**Phantom Membranes** - 32x at an early milestone.\
**Experience Bottles** - Multiple XP bottle drops at higher tiers.

***

### Premium Pass Rewards

The premium track runs for all 60 tiers with rewards at nearly every single level. The focus is heavily on vbits with 0.25 vbits at almost every tier, plus larger milestone rewards including:

**Every tier** - 0.25x vbits.\
**Tier 2** - 1x Ultra Rare Crate Key + 0.25x vbits.\
**Tier 7** - $5,000 Cash + 0.25x vbits.\
**Tier 8** - 1x Rare Crate Key + 0.25x vbits.\
**Tier 14** - 1x Cosmetic Crate Key + 0.25x vbits.\
**Tier 18** - $5,000 Cash + 0.25x vbits.\
**Tier 26** - $3,000 Cash + 0.25x vbits.\
**Tier 32** - 1x Cosmetic Crate Key + 0.25x vbits.\
**Tier 39** - 1x Rare Crate Key + 0.25x vbits.\
**Tier 44** - 1x Ultra Rare Crate Key + 0.25x vbits.\
**Tier 60** - 5x vbits + 1x Rune Crate Key.

A full premium season delivers a total of roughly 15+ vbits just from the 0.25 per tier payouts alone, plus all the milestone bonuses on top.

***

### Commands

✅ /battlepass - Open your Battle Pass menu to view quests, tiers, and claim rewards.\
✅ /battlepass quests - View your active quests.\
✅ /battlepass rewards - Open the rewards menu directly.\
✅ /battlepass leaderboard - See the top players for the current season.

***

### Tips and Good to Know

⭐ Rewards are not claimed automatically. Open /battlepass regularly and collect what you have earned.\
⭐ Daily quests reset at noon every day. Try to complete all nine before reset to maximize your points.\
⭐ You do not need the premium pass to progress. Free players reach tier 60 the same way premium players do, just with different rewards waiting at each tier.\
⭐ The premium track pays out 0.25 vbits at almost every single tier making it one of the most efficient vbits sources on the server.\
⭐ The Monthly Crate Key at premium tier 60 is the monthly crate key, so completing the pass guarantees you a shot at the current monthly set.
