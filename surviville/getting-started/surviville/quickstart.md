# Ranks

## Free Tier:

**Mortal** - 2 houses, 2 warps. 2 vaults\
**Wanderer** - 3 houses, 3 warps. 3 vaults\
**Rogue** - 4 houses, 4 warps. 4vaults

## Sovereign

#### **Commands:**

✅ **/anvil** – Instantly repair your items on the go.\
✅ **/condense** – Convert raw materials into their compact forms with ease.\
✅ **/disposal (/trash)** – Open a convenient GUI to clear out unwanted items.\
✅ **/enderchest** – Access your ender chest anytime, anywhere.\
✅ **/beezooka** – Launch a bee at your target—whether friend or foe, we won’t judge.\
✅ **/kittycannon** – Is someone getting catty? Send an explosive feline their way.\
✅ **/nick** – Customize your in-game name with full hex and gradient support using BirdFlop.\
✅ **/recipe** – Instantly check any crafting recipe without searching online.\
✅ **/feed** – Keep your hunger bar full without carrying food.

#### **Additional Perks:**

⭐ Custom chat prefix to display your rank.\
⭐ 10 personal vaults for extra storage (/pv).\
⭐ Set up to 8 homes for quick teleportation.
