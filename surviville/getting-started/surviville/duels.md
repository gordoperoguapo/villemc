# Duels

## Duels

Want to settle a score? Challenge another player to a 1v1 fight in a dedicated arena. Duels on Surviville are high stakes. You fight with your own gear, and the winner gets to loot the loser's drops. Come prepared.

***

### How It Works

Duels take place in separate arenas away from the main world. When a duel starts, both players are teleported into the arena and a countdown begins. A shrinking border closes in over time so there is no camping or running. The arena resets automatically after every match so the next fight always starts fresh.

There are two ways to get into a duel. You can challenge a specific player directly, or you can join the queue and get matched against a random opponent.

***

### This Is a Risk Fight

Your inventory is **not protected** in a duel. You fight with whatever gear you are currently wearing and carrying. When a player dies, their items drop on the ground and the winner has a short window to collect the loot before it disappears. Only enter a duel if you are willing to lose what you have on you.

***

### Commands

#### Challenging a Player

✅ `/duel <player>` - Send a duel request to a specific player. A menu will open letting you choose the arena and match time.\
✅ `/duel accept <player>` - Accept an incoming duel request.\
✅ `/duel decline <player>` - Decline an incoming duel request.\
✅ `/duel viewrequest <player>` - View the details of an incoming duel request before accepting.

#### Queue

✅ `/duel` - Open the queue menu to join casual matchmaking and get paired against a random opponent automatically.

#### During a Match

✅ `/draw` - Request a draw with your opponent. Both players must agree for the match to end in a tie.

#### Stats

✅ `/duel stats` - View your personal duel stats including wins, losses, and win streak.\
✅ `/duel top` - View the top players on the duels leaderboard.

#### Spectating

✅ `/spectate <player>` - Spectate an ongoing duel that a player is currently in.\
✅ `/spectate leave` - Exit spectator mode and return to spawn.

#### Other

✅ `/dueltoggle` - Toggle whether other players can send you duel requests.

***

### Arenas

Duels take place across a variety of unique handbuilt arenas. The arena is selected when sending a challenge, or assigned randomly in the queue. Current arenas include Bionic, Skull, Magic, Crystal, Mushroom, Pirate, Hostile Garden, Snow, Waterfall Forest, Wasteland, Candy, Aquatic, Cobra Nest, Volcano, and Dinosaur.

***

### Tips and Good to Know

⭐ You fight with your own gear, so only duel if you are comfortable with the risk of losing your items.\
⭐ The winner has a limited time after the fight to collect loot from the arena before it disappears, so move fast.\
⭐ The border shrinks as the match goes on so camping in a corner is not a viable strategy.\
⭐ Logging out or disconnecting mid-duel counts as a loss and your items will be dropped.\
⭐ Most commands like /home, /spawn, and /tpa are blocked while you are in a match.\
⭐ If you do not want to be challenged right now, use /dueltoggle to pause incoming requests.\
⭐ You can request a draw using /draw if both players agree to end the match in a tie.
