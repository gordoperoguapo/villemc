# Hunting

## Hunting

Surviville is home to a wide variety of custom wildlife that roams the world naturally. Hunting these animals is one of the most engaging ways to explore the map, earn loot, and compete with other players on the leaderboard. (BEDROCK SUPPORT COMING SOON)\

### How It Works

Custom wildlife spawns naturally throughout the world in biome-specific locations. Animals have custom models, sounds, and behaviors. Most are passive and will flee when approached, but some will fight back if provoked. When you kill an animal it drops loot specific to that mob, and every kill counts toward your total on the hunting leaderboard.

***

### Wildlife and Where to Find Them

Animals spawn in biomes that match their natural habitat. No two animals share a cap, so multiple species can coexist in the same area.

**Bison** - Plains, Sunflower Plains, Meadow, Savanna. Aggressive when provoked. High health and knockback resistance. Drops Beef and Leather.\
**Bear** - Taiga, Old Growth Pine Taiga, Snowy Taiga. Aggressive. Tough fight with high damage. Drops Beef and Leather.\
**Boar** - Forest, Birch Forest, Jungle. Aggressive. Fast and dangerous in groups. Drops Beef and Leather.\
**Deer** - Forest, Birch Forest, Meadow, Plains. Passive and fast. Drops Beef and Leather.\
**Moose** - Taiga, Old Growth Pine Taiga, Snowy Taiga, Swamp, River. Aggressive. Drops Beef and Leather. Bow kills reward a bonus crate key.\
**Lynx** - Taiga, Old Growth Pine Taiga, Snowy Taiga, Grove. Aggressive. Drops Rabbit Hide and Leather.\
**Marten** - Forest, Birch Forest, Old Growth Birch Forest, Taiga. Passive. Drops Rabbit Hide.\
**Beaver** - River, Swamp, Mangrove Swamp. Passive and flees quickly. Drops Spruce Log and Rabbit Hide.\
**Otter** - River, Swamp, Mangrove Swamp, Beach. Passive. Drops Rabbit Hide.\
**Badger** - Forest, Birch Forest, Dark Forest. Passive. Drops Rabbit Hide.

***

### Wildlife Pack Mobs

In addition to the core wildlife above, a second group of ambient creatures roams the world. These are rarer and more spread out, capping at one per chunk.

**Bears** - Black, Brown, Panda, Polar, and Water variants across various biomes.\
**Crabs** - Hermit, Mudflat, Red, and Water variants near beaches and rivers.\
**Crocodiles** - Cuban, Saltwater, and Water variants in swamps and rivers.\
**Ducks** - Mallard, Water, and White variants near water.\
**Squirrels** - Brown, Gray, Log, and Red variants in forests.\
**Rats** - Black, Brown, and Gray variants in dark areas.\
**Fireflies** - Ambient in swamps and forests at night.

All of these count toward your /huntingtop total.

***

### Hunting Tips

✅ Bring a bow. Wildlife reward a bonus crate key on bow kills only so ranged combat pays off.\
✅ Aggressive mobs like Bears, Bison, Boar, and Moose hit hard. Come prepared with good gear.\
✅ Passive mobs flee fast. Having a horse or sprint gear helps you keep up.\
✅ Wildlife caps at 2 per species within 64 blocks so spreading out gives you more to hunt.\
✅ Every kill of every tracked animal adds to your total for /huntingtop.

***

### Hunting Leaderboard

Use /huntingtop to open the hunting leaderboard GUI. It shows the top players ranked by total wildlife kills across all tracked animals. Hover over any player on the board to see a full breakdown of exactly which animals they have killed and how many of each.

All core wildlife and wildlife pack mobs count toward your total. Goblins and other non-wildlife mobs do not count.

***

### Tips and Good to Know

⭐ Biomes with multiple compatible species like Taiga and Forest give you the most variety in a single area.\
⭐ Wildlife does not respawn instantly. There is a per-species cooldown before a new one can spawn in the same area so move around to find more.\
⭐ Moose are one of the harder fights on the server. High health, high damage, and they chase you. Bring armor.\
⭐ Beavers and Otters are passive but drop useful materials. Fast and worth hunting if you need Rabbit Hide.\
⭐ The hunting leaderboard is permanent and competitive. Every kill counts so keep hunting.
