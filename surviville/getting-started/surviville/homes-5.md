# Raidboss

## World Boss

Three times a day a powerful boss spawns on Surviville for the entire server to fight together. It is one of the best ways to earn rewards and it scales to however many players show up so there is no such thing as too many people joining in.\
<br>

***

### Schedule

The boss spawns automatically at the following times every day. Warning broadcasts go out at 30 minutes, 15 minutes, 5 minutes, and 1 minute before each spawn so you always have time to head over.

⏰ 12:00\
⏰ 18:00\
⏰ 21:00

The boss despawns after 1 hour if it has not been killed. Teleport to the boss arena using **/raid**.

***

### How It Works

When the boss spawns everyone on the server is invited to participate. Deal damage, tank hits, or apply debuffs and status effects to contribute. Your contribution is tracked across three categories and rewards are given out when the boss dies based on how well you performed in each.

The boss has a leash radius so it will not wander far from its spawn point. Stay in the arena to keep it engaged.

***

### Reward Categories

Rewards are split into three tracks. You can place in more than one category so playing aggressively and tanking hits both benefit you.

#### Damage

Awarded to the top players who dealt the most damage to the boss.

🥇 1st Place - 5x Reward Crate Keys + Boss Loot Table drop\
🥈 2nd Place - $3,000 Cash + 10x Gold Ingots\
🥉 3rd Place - $1,000 Cash + 15x Iron Ingots

The killing blow also grants a 1.5x bonus multiplier on top of your normal reward.

#### Tank

Awarded to the top players who absorbed the most damage from the boss.

🥇 1st Place - $3,000 Cash + 2x Netherite Scrap\
🥈 2nd Place - $1,500 Cash + 3x Golden Apples

#### Support

Awarded to the top players who applied the most debuffs and status effects to the boss.

🥇 1st Place - $2,000 Cash + 5x Emeralds\
🥈 2nd Place - $1,000 Cash + 10x Lapis Lazuli

***

### Loot Protection

When the boss dies, dropped items are protected for 60 seconds and can only be picked up by the players who earned them. After 60 seconds any uncollected items become public and anyone can grab them so make sure you collect your drops quickly.

***

### Commands

✅ `/raid` - Teleport to the boss arena.\
✅ `/raid info` - Check the current boss status and time until the next spawn.\
✅ `/raid stats [player]` - View your personal boss statistics including total damage, kills, participations, and leaderboard finishes.\
✅ `/raidtop` - Quick access to the raid leaderboard.

***

### Tips and Good to Know

⭐ You do not need to be the top damage dealer to earn rewards. Tanking hits and applying debuffs both have their own prize tracks.\
⭐ Warnings go out at 30, 15, 5, and 1 minute before each spawn. Keep an eye on chat so you do not miss it.\
⭐ The killing blow gives a 1.5x reward bonus on top of the normal damage reward so finishing the boss is worth going for.\
⭐ If you miss a session and had pending rewards from a previous fight they will be delivered to you automatically when you next log in.\
⭐ The boss rotates periodically so the fight will look different each season.
