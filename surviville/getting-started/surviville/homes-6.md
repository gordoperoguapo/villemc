# Shop

## Shop

The shop is where you buy and sell almost everything on Surviville. Open it anywhere with `/shop` or `/market`. Use `/sellall` to instantly sell everything in your inventory that has a sell price.\

***

### Categories

The shop is split into six categories.

**Mob Drops** - Items dropped by mobs including bones, blaze rods, ender pearls, gunpowder, slimeballs, phantom membranes, ghast tears, and more.\
**Ores** - All ores and minerals including coal, iron, gold, diamonds, emeralds, lapis, redstone, copper, amethyst, quartz, ancient debris, and netherite.\
**Blocks** - Building blocks including stone, logs, sand, wool, glass, terracotta, deepslate, nether blocks, end stone, and many more.\
**Farming** - Crops and farm goods including custom and vanilla crops/items.\
**Spawners** - Custom spawners for Chicken, Pig, Sheep, Cow, Spider, Skeleton, Creeper, Enderman, and Iron Golem.\
**Miscellaneous** - Fishing rods, bait, and hooks for the custom fishing system.

***

### Commands

✅ `/shop` - Open the main shop menu.\
✅ `/market` - Alias for /shop.\
✅ `/sellall` - Instantly sell everything in your inventory that has a sell price.\
✅ `/sell` - Open the sell GUI to manually place items and sell them.

***

### Selling

Most items in the shop can be sold back for money. The sell price is always lower than the buy price so selling is best done with items you gathered yourself rather than items you bought.

The fastest way to sell is `/sellall` which sweeps your entire inventory and pays you out instantly for anything with a sell value. You can also use `/sell` to manually choose what to sell.

***

Tips and Good to Know

⭐ Use `/sellall` after every mining or farming session to cash out quickly without opening any menus.\
⭐ Some items sell for more than their buy price so check the shop before assuming you should sell elsewhere.\
⭐ Spawners cannot be sold back so make sure you want one before purchasing.\
⭐ Farming high-value crops like Garlic and Cabbage and selling via /sellall is one of the fastest early-game money methods.
