# In-Game Rewards

/rewards - Opens up the rewards menu



each rank gets a different tier of key allocated to them after a certain period of gameplay. Each tier stacks depending on rank level.

***

### Different Tiers:

⭐ **Player Rank**

1x Reward Key every 1 hour of playtime

⭐ **Sovereign Rank**

1x Reward Key every 1 hour of playtime\
\
⭐ **Radiant Rank**\
\
1x Reward Key every 1.5 hours of playtime\
\
**⭐ Mythic Rank**

1x Rare Key every 24 hours of playtime

**⭐ Eternal Rank**

1x Ultra Rare Key every 72 hours of playtime
